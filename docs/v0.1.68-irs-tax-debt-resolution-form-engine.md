# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.68

## IRS Tax Debt Resolution Form Engine — Controlled Phase 1

This release imports the uploaded IRS tax-debt/problem-resolution PDFs as controlled source assets, records SHA-256 checksums, establishes a 17-form resolution catalog, adds plain-English routing logic, exposes controlled library/questionnaire/recommendation endpoints, and adds staff mapping and QA foundations.

### Preserved safety boundaries
- Live sensitive taxpayer uploads remain blocked.
- Captured PDFs are not automatically final-output ready.
- No e-file, IRS transmission, settlement, eligibility, or approval claim is made.
- Forms requiring professional judgment are routed for professional review.

### Remaining work
Each form still requires official source URL/revision verification, exact field inventory or coordinate mapping, sample-filled generation, page-by-page visual QA, overflow/checkbox/date/signature testing, client verification, and professional release before controlled final output.
