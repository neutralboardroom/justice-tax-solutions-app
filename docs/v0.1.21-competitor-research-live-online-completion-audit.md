# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.21 Competitor Research + Live Online Completion Audit

v0.1.21 adds a current competitor-research layer and turns the live online professional consultation/completion idea into a productized platform path.

## Competitor lessons added

The build now tracks major categories:

- TurboTax / Intuit premium DIY, Expert Assist, Expert Full Service, Intuit Assist, and store/hybrid expansion.
- H&R Block Online, Tax Pro Review, AI Tax Assist, office/pro model, Block Advisors, Jackson Hewitt, and Liberty-style hybrid services.
- Low-cost/free DIY competitors including FreeTaxUSA, TaxAct, TaxSlayer, Cash App Taxes, and eFile.com.
- Tax-resolution firms such as Optima, TaxRise, Community Tax, Tax Defense Network, and local EA/CPA/tax attorney firms.
- AI and embedded tax firms including april, Column Tax, Keeper, FlyFin, TaxGPT, Instead, Filed, Blue J, and CoCounsel Tax.
- Government/free alternatives including IRS Free File, VITA/TCE, state portals, and the former IRS Direct File model.

## Live online completion model

New service path:

- AI Completion Only.
- Live Online PTIN Completion.
- Live Online EA Tax Problem Session.
- Live Online CPA Business/Gig Review.
- Live Online Tax Attorney Strategy Review.
- Full Online Completion With Professional.

Design rule: AI prepares the interview, missing-info list, source-linked field candidates, draft forms, and professional agenda before a live professional session. Human time should be used only for credential-required review, judgment, correction, explanation, and release approval.

## Guardrails preserved

- AI-only is not professional review.
- Legal advice requires licensed tax attorney review and a separate appropriate engagement scope.
- No guarantee of refunds, debt reduction, OIC acceptance, agency outcome, or same-day representation.
- Final official form output still requires source capture, checksum, field mapping, sample-fill QA, overflow/signature/calculation checks, client verification, and required review.
- Live sensitive taxpayer document handling remains blocked until production security gates are configured.
