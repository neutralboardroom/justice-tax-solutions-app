# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.73

## Purpose

Make prior-return review and amendment-opportunity screening unmistakably separate from filing a new or unfiled return, without redesigning or disrupting the established homepage flow.

## User problem solved

A taxpayer who already filed has a different intent from a taxpayer who still needs to file. Combining those intentions can hide one of Justice Tax Solutions’ strongest offers: reviewing prior personal or business returns to determine whether an amended return may be worth considering.

## Changes

- Renamed the homepage chooser heading to “Choose the situation that fits best.”
- Reordered the six existing paths around the user’s immediate situation.
- Added a distinct featured path: “I already filed and want a second look.”
- Kept personal-return filing and business-return filing as separate paths.
- Refined the dedicated prior-return homepage section to explain:
  - AI-assisted organization and issue flagging;
  - human tax review;
  - the free initial amendment-opportunity screening;
  - possible review areas;
  - paid work only after scope is explained; and
  - no guarantee of refund, savings, acceptance, or result.
- Updated the intake selector wording while preserving the existing `past-return-review` route key.

## Preserved

- The six core public paths and all existing route keys.
- Existing personal and business filing flows.
- Free Tax Problem Truth Check and tax-problem routing.
- The dedicated Review Past Returns page and Form 1040-X resources.
- AI/form-engine functionality and controlled release gates.
- Live sensitive-upload restrictions.
- No deployment was performed.

## Readiness effect

This is a public clarity and conversion improvement. It does not change form mapping, calculation, professional approval, sensitive-document handling, payment, filing, or e-file readiness.
