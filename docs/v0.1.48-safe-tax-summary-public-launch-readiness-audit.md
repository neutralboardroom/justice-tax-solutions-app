# v0.1.48 Safe Tax Summary + Public Launch Readiness Audit

## Purpose

This pass adds a public Safe Tax Summary Builder so first real users can describe a tax issue without uploading or pasting sensitive taxpayer documents. It also adds a final public launch readiness checklist that separates what can be used now from what remains blocked before broad public launch.

## Added

- `/safe-tax-summary-builder.html`
- `GET /api/tax/safe-tax-summary-builder-guide`
- `POST /api/tax/safe-tax-summary`
- `GET /api/platform/public-launch-final-readiness-checklist`

## Preserved launch blocks

- No real unredacted taxpayer documents.
- No final official IRS/NYS/NYC form output.
- No e-file or agency-submission claims.
- No guaranteed tax outcomes.
- No broad paid marketing until external operations are complete.

## Remaining full-launch gates

- Domain/SSL and `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` custom-domain update.
- Transactional email.
- Stripe live checkout and webhook.
- Professional roster/PTIN/EA/CPA/attorney/NY compliance review.
- Managed database, private storage, malware scanning, staff MFA, WISP, retention, incident response, and backup/restore testing.
- Official PDF capture/checksum/coordinate/sample-fill/professional signoff before client-ready official forms.
- Authorized e-file provider/EFIN path before e-file or agency submission claims.

## Audit conclusion

v0.1.48 is suitable for public website review and controlled first-user use in a no-file/redacted-sample mode. It is not suitable for unrestricted public launch, live sensitive documents, final official forms, or e-file claims.
