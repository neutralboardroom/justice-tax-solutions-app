# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.63 - Deployment-safe persistence and save/resume continuity

This non-form build adds a data-continuity layer so Justice Tax Solutions deployments are treated as source-code updates only, never data resets.

## Core rule

Every new deployment must preserve:

- customer accounts and login details
- case records and partial intake progress
- uploaded document records and encrypted/private storage references
- staff notes, tasks, assignments, triage status, and follow-up dates
- accountant/CPA/EA/bookkeeper/payroll/sales-tax/tax attorney work and signoffs
- quotes, payment records, Stripe/session metadata, receipts, refund/chargeback history
- referral attribution and rewards
- audit/access/release events

## Safety posture

No new IRS form bundles were added. Live sensitive taxpayer uploads, final official form output, e-file, agency submission, direct debit, Refund Transfer, and refund advances remain blocked.
