# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.32 — IRS Form 9465 Fill/Overlay Engine Prep Audit

## Scope

v0.1.32 adds the first actual fill/overlay engine preparation layer for IRS Form 9465. It converts validated interview answers and calculated fields into output-value candidates, joins them to drafted overlay coordinates, generates internal sample overlay PDFs for visual QA, and adds staff fill-engine status tracking.

## What changed

- Added `src/form9465FillEngine.js`.
- Added fill-engine readiness gates.
- Added overlay template endpoint.
- Added fill-plan endpoint.
- Added sample filled overlay QA PDF endpoint.
- Added case-level fill plan and sample overlay endpoints.
- Added staff fill-engine board and status endpoints.
- Updated Official Forms dashboard copy and markers.

## Release gate

Final client-facing IRS Form 9465 output remains blocked. The engine is now ready to prepare samples and QA placement, but official final output still requires:

1. official PDF capture/upload from the official IRS source;
2. checksum record;
3. AcroForm field or overlay-coordinate lock;
4. sample filled output;
5. visual QA;
6. overflow/date/money/checkbox QA;
7. signature blank verification;
8. direct debit client confirmation or blank fields;
9. client verification;
10. required professional/staff release signoff;
11. production security/payment/release gates.

## Safety decision

Generated sample overlay PDFs are marked internal QA only and are not official IRS forms. They are coordinate/field-placement QA sheets to compare against the captured official PDF before final client output is enabled.
