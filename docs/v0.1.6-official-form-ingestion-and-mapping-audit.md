# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.6 official form ingestion and mapping audit

## Purpose

v0.1.6 prepares Justice Tax Solutions for official IRS, New York State, and New York City form ZIP uploads. This is the bridge between the intake/document-intelligence platform and future real form completion.

## What was added

- Official form ZIP ingestion module: `src/officialForms.js`
- ZIP parsing without adding a new dependency
- PDF-only extraction from uploaded official form archives
- Official form package records
- Official form catalog records
- SHA-256 checksums for archive and extracted PDFs
- Agency inference for IRS, NYS, and NYC filenames
- Tax year inference where available
- Form-number heuristics for common IRS/NYS/NYC forms
- Official source status based on source URL/domain hints
- First-wave mapping priority labels
- Staff mapping queue
- Mapping draft generator
- Staff mapping status updates
- Staff official-form download endpoint with access logging
- Official form upload UI at `/official-forms.html`
- PostgreSQL schema additions for official form packages, official forms, mappings, and field maps

## Safety rule

A PDF being uploaded does not mean it is ready for client use. Every official form must pass:

1. Source verification
2. Tax year / edition-date verification
3. Field-name extraction or mapping
4. Plain-English question mapping
5. Calculation/cross-form dependency review
6. Sample PDF output testing
7. Professional review workflow confirmation

Only then can the form move to `mapped_verified` and become usable after case-specific review.

## Upload order recommendation

1. IRS tax-problem/debt forms and instructions: 9465, 433-F, 433-A, 433-B, 656, 2848, 8821, 1040-X, 4868.
2. IRS individual return foundation: 1040, schedules 1/2/3, A/B/C/D/SE, credits, 8962, 8889.
3. New York State individual foundation: IT-201, IT-203, IT-2, IT-196, IT-201-X, IT-203-X, IT-370, IT-2105, major credits.
4. NYC business/freelancer forms: NYC-202, NYC-202S, NYC-204, NYC-204EZ, NYC-221, NYC-EXT.

## Current limitations

- ZIP parser supports standard ZIP entries with stored or deflated files, not encrypted archives.
- It catalogs PDF files and does not yet extract actual AcroForm field names.
- It does not yet generate filled PDFs.
- It does not yet validate PDF edition dates automatically.
- It does not yet compare official PDFs against the agency live page.
- Mapping is a workflow foundation, not final tax form automation.
