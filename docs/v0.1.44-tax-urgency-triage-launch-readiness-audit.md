# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.44 — Tax Urgency Triage + Launch Readiness Alignment Audit

This pass audits v0.1.43 and makes another launch-safe user-value improvement without enabling live sensitive taxpayer documents, final official form output, e-file, agency submission, guaranteed outcomes, or broad paid marketing.

## What changed

1. Added `/tax-urgency-triage.html` so public users can check urgency signals before uploading, paying, calling, signing, or responding.
2. Added `src/taxUrgencyTriage.js` with a safe first-step routing model for IRS/NYS/NYC notice and collection signals.
3. Added `GET /api/tax/urgency-triage-guide` and `POST /api/tax/urgency-triage`.
4. Updated homepage, launch-readiness, and launch-audit pages to include the urgency triage path.
5. Fixed stale public-launch audit packaging logic that still expected v0.1.41 after later builds.
6. Updated version markers and static smoke tests to v0.1.44.

## Safety limits

The urgency triage is not legal, tax, accounting, filing, or agency-response advice. It does not calculate official deadlines. It only helps users organize facts and choose a safer next step.

## Launch interpretation after v0.1.44

- Public website viewing: yes, after deployment/domain/SSL smoke test.
- Invite-only no-sensitive pilot: yes, with no-file or redacted/sample documents only.
- Controlled paid pilot: not yet.
- Broad marketing: not yet.
- Live sensitive taxpayer uploads: no.
- Final official form output: no.
- E-file / agency submission claims: no.
