# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.75 — Credits and Dependents Controlled Form Engine

## Purpose

Integrate the user-supplied official IRS credits/dependents archive and create fail-closed workflows for Schedule EIC and Forms 8332, 8862, 8867, and 8880.

## Official assets added

- 2025 Schedule EIC (Form 1040)
- Form 8332 (Rev. December 2025)
- Form 8862 (Rev. December 2025)
- Instructions for Form 8862 (Rev. December 2025)
- Form 8867 (Rev. November 2024)
- Instructions for Form 8867 (Rev. November 2025)
- 2025 Form 8880

Each asset has a packaged SHA-256 checksum, page count, field count, official IRS URL, revision/tax-year metadata, and a release-blocked status.

## Controlled workflows

### Schedule EIC

- Collects up to three qualifying-child records.
- Maps return name/SSN, child name/SSN, birth year, conditional age/student/disability answers, relationship, and months.
- Requires a qualified reviewer to confirm EIC eligibility and determine the credit amount separately.
- Does not calculate EIC.
- Supports synthetic staff-only sample PDF generation.

### Form 8880

- Supports 2025 whole-dollar calculations for the form’s lines 1–10 and line 12.
- Uses the 2025 line 9 rate table.
- Requires line 11 from a separately reviewed Credit Limit Worksheet.
- Stops on unsupported age, dependent, student, foreign-income/Puerto Rico/American Samoa adjustments, or incomplete facts.
- Supports synthetic staff-only sample PDF generation.

### Forms 8332, 8862, and 8867

- Form 8332 is organizer-only and signature-controlled.
- Form 8862 is professional-review intake for prior credit disallowance.
- Form 8867 is a preparer-only due-diligence workflow and may not be certified by AI.

## Release posture

This version is appropriate for official-source intake, internal routing, deterministic synthetic tests, and controlled sample-output QA. It is not approved for real taxpayer data, final filing output, e-file, agency submission, or unrestricted paying users.
