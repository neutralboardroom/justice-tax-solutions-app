# v0.1.51 Final Controlled Public Launch Closeout + Deployment Preparation

## Purpose

v0.1.51 closes out the controlled public launch preparation work without pretending the platform is fully production-ready. It keeps Justice Tax Solutions tax-only and tax-problem-first, focused on IRS/NYS/NYC notices, tax debt, unfiled/prior-year problems, amended returns, self-employed/gig-worker tax issues, and safe tax-return review workflows.

## What changed

- Added `src/controlledLaunchCloseout.js` with final go/no-go, deployment-prep, public-navigation, first-cohort staff operations, official source-freshness, and privacy-safe analytics audits.
- Added public endpoints for the new closeout/readiness material.
- Added `/controlled-public-launch-closeout.html` and `/staff-pilot-ops.html`.
- Updated `/launch-readiness.html` to surface v0.1.51 closeout status.
- Reduced customer-facing homepage clutter and moved internal launch/roadmap links out of the hero.
- Preserved every no-sensitive launch gate and Form 9465 final-output block.
- Updated version/package/test markers to v0.1.51.

## Preserved safety rules

- No real unredacted taxpayer document uploads.
- No final official IRS/NYS/NYC form output.
- No e-file or agency-submission claims.
- No direct-debit readiness claim.
- No guaranteed refund, settlement, payment plan, penalty relief, OIC, audit, or agency outcome.
- No professional review promise unless credentials/scope/payment/security gates are verified.
- Environment/status pages show booleans/status only, never secret values.

## Readiness judgment

- Public website viewing: conditionally ready after live deployment, HTTPS, and smoke checks.
- Invited first real users: ready only for no-file or redacted/sample-only controlled pilot.
- No-file intake: ready.
- Redacted/sample intake: ready when acknowledgment is checked and `ALLOW_SAMPLE_UPLOADS` is true.
- Controlled paid pilot: not ready until Stripe live keys/webhook/products/prices, email, SOPs, and owner approval are tested.
- Broad marketing: not ready.
- Live sensitive uploads: blocked.
- Final official form output: blocked.
- E-file/agency-submission claims: blocked.

Managed PostgreSQL, private object storage, malware scanning/quarantine, production email, live Stripe/webhook testing, staff/professional MFA controls, verified professional roster, PTIN/EA/CPA/tax attorney credential controls, NY preparer compliance review, WISP/security plan approval, retention/deletion policy, incident response, backup/restore testing, official form QA, e-file/EFIN path, and final owner security approval.
