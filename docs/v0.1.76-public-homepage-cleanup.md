# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.76

## Purpose

Refine the public homepage after the v0.1.75 launch without changing the approved logo, color palette, typography, core routes, form engines, or safety posture.

## Improvements

- Removed staff, owner, QA, release-continuity, deployment, and version-history material from the customer homepage.
- Shortened the homepage to five public sections: hero/path selection, prior-return review, how it works, start form, and trust/safety cards.
- Removed Data Continuity from public navigation and renamed Free Truth Check navigation to the clearer Tax problems label while preserving its route.
- Replaced the oversized four-card intake sidebar with a compact sticky guide that updates when the user changes paths.
- Reduced six always-visible consent checkboxes to two primary acknowledgments, plus an upload acknowledgment that appears only when files are selected.
- Preserved backend consent fields and upload safety validation through synchronized form controls.
- Removed public version labels and internal phrases such as controlled maps, platform polish audit, official PDF intake, and pilot operations.
- Kept save/resume available through the account/dashboard route without promoting operational continuity controls to public users.

## Preserved

- Existing logo, favicon, colors, fonts, and card styling.
- Six-path intake architecture.
- Prior-return and amendment-review prominence.
- Personal and business filing paths.
- Tax notice and tax debt paths.
- Free starting summary and free initial amendment screening language.
- Required independence, no-guarantee, AI-limitation, urgent-matter, and upload acknowledgments.
- All official IRS form assets, mappings, controlled engines, release gates, and security restrictions.
