# v0.1.18 AI-First Official Form Completion Audit

## Purpose

This build implements the owner direction that Justice Tax Solutions should use AI and platform logic to complete official tax forms as fully as possible, ask follow-up questions only when facts require them, and reduce human work to exception review.

## Added

- `src/formAutomation.js` with AI-first completion policy, pipeline, interview templates, validation, field candidate planning, sample-fill audit planning, and exception review queue logic.
- First-wave form interviews for IRS 9465, IRS 2848, IRS 8821, IRS 433-F, IRS 1040-X, NYS IT-201, and NYC-202.
- Public/API endpoints for form interview, answer checks, sample-fill audit planning, and automation roadmap.
- Case-specific form completion plan endpoints that store merged form answers and generate output gates.
- Staff exception review queue focused on minimizing manual work while still flagging high-risk, professional, legal/tax judgment, and unsupported output issues.
- Official forms page now displays the AI-first completion roadmap and first-wave interview templates.

## Guardrails preserved

- AI drafts field candidates and completion plans, but final print/signature output stays blocked until official PDF capture, field-map verification, sample-fill QA, client verification, and required exception review are complete.
- Government-only, preparer-only, signature, and unsupported fields stay controlled.
- High-risk tax debt, bankruptcy, large balances, collection disclosure, amended filing-status/dependent changes, representative eligibility, and NYC allocation issues trigger exception review.
- Sensitive taxpayer uploads remain gated by production configuration.
