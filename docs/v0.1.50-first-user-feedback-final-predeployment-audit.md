This pass keeps the platform in controlled real-user launch mode. It adds a first-user feedback loop so the owner and staff can learn from the first invited users before broader public traffic.

## Still blocked

- Real unredacted taxpayer document uploads
- Final official IRS/NYS/NYC tax form output
- E-file or agency-submission claims
- Broad paid marketing

## Operating rule

Do not invite a larger cohort until staff reviews first-user feedback and fixes repeated confusion around uploads, independence from government/law firms, pricing, review level, urgency, after-start expectations, and final-action limitations.
