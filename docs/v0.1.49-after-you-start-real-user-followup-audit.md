# v0.1.49 After-You-Start + First-Case Follow-Up Audit

## Purpose

This build adds the next practical user-value layer for controlled real-user launch: users now get a clear explanation of what happens after they start, and staff get a first-case follow-up board for safe pilot handling.

## What changed

- Added `/after-you-start.html`.
- Added `/api/tax/after-you-start-guide`.
- Added `/api/tax/post-submit-expectation-check`.
- Added `/api/staff/first-case-followup-board`.
- Added post-submit expectation checking for final-action, urgency, business-tax, sensitive-data, and paid/professional-review signals.
- Added staff follow-up SLA, no-sensitive email templates, and review-before-next-cohort rules.

## Preserved safety posture

- Live sensitive uploads remain blocked.
- Redacted/sample-only pilot mode remains the safe user path.
- Official tax-form output remains controlled QA only.
- E-file, direct debit, signature, agency submission, and representation claims remain blocked.
- Users are repeatedly reminded that Justice Tax Solutions is not the IRS, NYS, NYC, or a law firm and does not guarantee outcomes.

## Remaining launch blockers

Full public launch still requires custom domain/SSL verification, transactional email, Stripe live payments and webhook testing, professional credential verification, NY preparer compliance review, WISP/security approval, managed database, private object storage, malware scanning, staff MFA/access controls, retention/deletion policy, incident response, backup/restore testing, official-form QA, and final owner approval.

## Readiness judgment

v0.1.49 is suitable for public website review and controlled first-user use in no-file/redacted-sample mode. It is not suitable for unrestricted public launch, live sensitive documents, final official forms, or e-file claims.
