# v0.1.52 IRS 433-F Controlled Organizer + Field Map

## Purpose

v0.1.52 begins the deeper IRS official-form buildout by adding a controlled Form 433-F organizer and logical field map. This is the next practical tax-problem-first bundle because IRS Form 433-F is commonly connected to payment-plan and collection cases and may be needed with Form 9465 in higher-balance or ability-to-pay situations.

## What was added

- New 433-F official source/policy module.
- Logical official field map by Form 433-F page and section.
- Public no-sensitive organizer page at `/irs-form-433-f-workspace.html`.
- Completion plan with required organizer fields, warnings, calculations, review triggers, and output gates.
- Readiness, field-map, organizer-schema, sample-case, completion-plan, audit, verification-sheet, and draft QA packet APIs.
- Official Forms dashboard block for 433-F readiness.

## What remains blocked

- Final official IRS Form 433-F output.
- Full SSN/EIN/account-number/signature handling in public mode.
- Agency submission, mailing instructions as complete, direct debit, representation, settlement, penalty-removal, payment-plan approval, or OIC claims.

## Next form/bundle recommendation

After testing v0.1.52, the next best bundle is either:

1. **IRS authorization/transcript bundle**: Form 8821 + Form 2848 + Form 4506-T, because staff/professionals may need tax information authorization and transcript workflows; or
2. **Penalty/notice response bundle**: Form 843 + notice-response organizer, because many anxious users need penalty-abatement or notice-response triage.

Keep building one small bundle at a time and preserve the controlled-launch/no-sensitive posture.
