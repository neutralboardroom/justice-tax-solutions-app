# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.25 First Controlled IRS Form 9465 Output Path Audit

## Scope
v0.1.25 adds the first controlled official-form output pathway for IRS Form 9465, Installment Agreement Request. The build is intentionally gated: it creates line-level field candidates, calculations, validation, sample QA packets, and client verification sheets, but it does not mark final client-facing official IRS output ready until official PDF capture, field/overlay confirmation, sample-fill QA, client verification, and required professional release are complete.

## New module
- `src/form9465Output.js`

## New capabilities
- Official IRS Form 9465 source metadata.
- Line-level 9465 field map covering Part I, signature areas, and conditional Part II.
- Calculations for lines 7, 9, and 10.
- Conditional Part II trigger logic.
- Form 433-F dependency and review trigger detection.
- Missing-answer and validation reporting.
- Client verification sheet generation.
- Internal/sample PDF QA packet generation with signature and final-output warnings.
- Staff QA status recording.
- Official output readiness report.

## Safety gates preserved
- No final official client-output release until source capture, checksum, field/overlay mapping, sample-fill QA, client verification, and professional/staff release are complete.
- Signature fields stay blank in AI-generated drafts.
- Direct debit banking fields require careful client verification.
- Amounts over $50,000 or low-payment facts trigger Form 433-F / collection review logic.
- Sample/internal PDFs are visibly labeled as QA/draft packets, not IRS submission receipts and not approval guarantees.

## Remaining blockers before final public 9465 output
- Capture/store official IRS PDF in production or attach a verified uploaded copy.
- Confirm whether the official PDF has fillable AcroForm fields or needs overlay-coordinate output.
- Lock field names/overlay coordinates for every line.
- Generate and inspect sample-filled official pages.
- Mark sample-fill QA as passed only after visual inspection.
- Add client-facing field-by-field verification UI.
- Require staff/professional release for cases with collection or Form 433-F triggers.
