# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.20 Review-Level Pricing Audit

v0.1.20 upgrades pricing from a simple AI/human/professional split into a five-level model:

1. AI Completion Only
2. PTIN Preparer Review
3. Enrolled Agent Review
4. CPA Review
5. Tax Attorney Review

The intent is to preserve the AI-first, human-minimal product strategy while making clear that higher credential levels are required when the facts demand it. The platform should always begin with the lowest safe level, then explain and obtain client approval if a case must upgrade.

## Core rules

- AI completes the full dynamic interview and asks follow-up questions when facts trigger more detail.
- AI prepares field candidates and print-ready draft packages only after official PDF capture, field mapping, sample-fill QA, and client verification gates are complete.
- AI Completion Only is not marketed as professional review, representation, legal advice, or guaranteed filing.
- Paid federal return preparation should route to PTIN preparer review where required.
- IRS controversy, tax debt, collection, OIC, or representation-sensitive matters should route to EA level or higher when beyond low-risk screening.
- Accounting-sensitive matters, business income, Schedule C, rentals, K-1s, entity returns, and NYS/NYC business tax issues should route to CPA level when complex.
- Legal-advice-sensitive matters should route to tax attorney review and may require a separate attorney engagement.

## Added endpoints

- `GET /api/platform/review-level-pricing`
- `POST /api/platform/review-level-routing`
- Updated `POST /api/platform/service-price-estimate` with `reviewLevel` support.

## Safety status

This version changes pricing and routing only. It does not remove official-form mapping gates, sensitive-upload gates, WISP/Publication 4557 safeguards, or professional/client verification gates.
