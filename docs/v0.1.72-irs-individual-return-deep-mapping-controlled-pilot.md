# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.72

## IRS Individual Return Deep Mapping and Controlled Pilot Preparation

### Purpose

This version begins the transition from a broad official-PDF inventory to a narrow, deterministic, explainable, and testable Form 1040 completion lane. It does not declare the full individual-return collection ready for real users.

### Official archive intake

Two user-supplied official-form archives were compared by filename role, form number, size, and SHA-256 checksum against the v0.1.71 packaged collections.

- Individual archive: 27 of 27 PDFs matched the packaged individual collection exactly.
- Resolution archive: 29 of 29 PDFs matched packaged counterparts exactly.
- The packaged resolution collection also contains Form 8821 and its instructions. They were preserved because removing them would regress the existing official-form library.
- Archive and document hash matches prove source-file identity only. They do not prove current revision, semantic mapping, tax accuracy, human QA, professional approval, or filing readiness.

The reproducible intake report is stored at `assets/official-forms/intake-reports/user-supplied-archives-v0.1.72.json`.

### First controlled lane

The first implementation is **2025 Form 1040 Simple W-2 Controlled Pilot**.

Supported in synthetic/internal-QA mode:

- tax year 2025;
- Single or Married Filing Jointly;
- one or more W-2s aggregated from box 1 and box 2;
- basic standard deduction;
- taxable income below $100,000;
- official 2025 Form 1040 tax-table lookup;
- deterministic refund or amount-owed calculation without direct deposit;
- exact controlled mapping into selected identity, address, filing-status, income, tax, payment, refund, and amount-owed fields.

Excluded and fail-closed:

- dependents and dependent-related credits;
- Earned Income Credit unless a qualified reviewer affirmatively confirms zero for the sample;
- itemized or additional deductions;
- age/blindness standard-deduction adjustments;
- self-employment, investment, rental, pass-through, retirement, Social Security, unemployment, or other income;
- Schedules 1, 2, or 3 amounts;
- digital-asset activity requiring reporting;
- estimated payments, refundable credits, foreign addresses, direct deposit, signatures, preparer fields, bank fields, filing, e-file, or agency submission;
- taxable income of $100,000 or more.

### AI-helper behavior

The new engine exposes a structured questionnaire, next-question state, validation results, unsupported-condition routing, a document checklist, deterministic calculations, a completion plan, and an engineered field-map report.

The helper must not:

- infer filing status or credit eligibility from incomplete facts;
- silently omit EIC or other tax benefits;
- log names, SSNs, addresses, W-2 values, or tax results;
- describe a generated PDF as signed, filed, accepted, e-filed, or professionally approved;
- continue inside the lane after an unsupported condition appears.

### Tax table

`tools/extract_2025_tax_table.py` reproducibly extracts the 2025 Form 1040 tax table from the packaged official instructions into `assets/tax-rules/2025-federal-income-tax-table.json`. The artifact contains 2,062 continuous intervals from $0 through $99,999 and validates the official example in which $25,300 of taxable income for Married Filing Jointly produces $2,562 of tax.

### PDF output controls

The sample generator:

- loads the packaged official 2025 Form 1040;
- populates only the controlled mapped fields;
- leaves signature, preparer, PTIN, routing-number, account-number, and direct-debit fields blank;
- adds a visible `SAMPLE / INTERNAL QA - NOT FOR FILING` watermark;
- writes metadata stating that the document is not signed, filed, accepted, or professionally approved;
- remains accessible only through a staff-protected draft endpoint.

### Current release status

This version is suitable for internal development and synthetic sample QA of the narrow lane. It is not approved for real taxpayer data, customer filing, final print-ready release, signatures, Stripe payment for this workflow, e-file, agency submission, or unrestricted public use.

Required gates still include human semantic verification, page-by-page human visual QA, qualified professional approval, owner release, production database and private storage, malware scanning, enforced MFA, approved WISP and retention/deletion procedures, tested backup/restore, and approved filing operations.
