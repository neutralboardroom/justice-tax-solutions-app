# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.22 Professional Session Readiness Audit

v0.1.22 adds an appointment-ready professional-session layer on top of the AI-first official form completion system.

## Product decision

Professionals can join for two reasons:

1. The case requires professional involvement because of paid-preparer rules, IRS collection risk, accounting complexity, legal sensitivity, or official-form release gates.
2. The customer requests professional reassurance, confidence, a second look, or full online completion even when AI-only might otherwise be possible.

The platform should not downgrade a customer who asks for a higher review level for reassurance. It should explain the scope and price before charging or confirming the session.

## Added capabilities

* Customer-facing professional session request path from the dashboard.
* Pre-session readiness scoring for each case.
* Missing basics and likely missing documents before scheduling.
* Professional-session products for reassurance checks, PTIN completion, EA tax-problem help, CPA business/gig review, tax attorney strategy, and full online completion.
* Quote/upgrade approval gate before confirming professional time.
* Staff professional-session board.
* Professional-room preview for staff/professionals with AI-prepared agenda, form plans, field plan, documents, release guardrails, and checklist.

## New endpoints

* `GET /api/platform/professional-session-products`
* `POST /api/platform/professional-session-estimate`
* `GET /api/cases/:id/pre-session-readiness`
* `POST /api/cases/:id/professional-session-request`
* `GET /api/staff/professional-session-board`
* `GET /api/staff/professional-room/:caseId`
* `POST /api/staff/professional-session-requests/:id/status`

## Guardrails

* AI-only remains a draft/organizer tier, not professional review or legal advice.
* Customer-requested reassurance is allowed, but must show scope, quote/upgrade status, and credential level.
* Official-form output remains blocked until source capture, checksum, field mapping, sample-fill QA, overflow/signature/calculation checks, client verification, and required professional verification are complete.
* Tax attorney review remains required for legal-advice-sensitive issues.
* No refund, relief, payment-plan, OIC, audit, timeline, or agency outcome is guaranteed.

## Still required before true production sessions

* Real calendar/availability integration.
* Video meeting link generation.
* Transactional email/SMS reminders.
* Staff/professional MFA and credential verification.
* Production storage, malware scanning, database, WISP/Publication 4557 safeguards, and operating procedures.
* Live Stripe checkout/webhook and clear upgrade-credit rules.
