# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.4 Competitor + Workflow Audit

## Purpose

This version studies legacy tax software, tax-resolution firms, professional tax software, practice-management/document-automation platforms, and newer AI tax platforms. The goal is not to copy them directly. The goal is to adapt the best mechanics for a tax-problem-first platform that also supports tax returns.

## Competitor groups reviewed

1. Consumer filing software: TurboTax, H&R Block, TaxAct, FreeTaxUSA, Jackson Hewitt Online, TaxSlayer, Cash App Taxes, Liberty Tax, eFile.com.
2. Tax-resolution firms: Optima Tax Relief, TaxRise, Community Tax, Anthem Tax Services, Tax Defense Network, Larson Tax Relief.
3. Professional tax software: Drake Tax, Intuit ProConnect, Lacerte, ProSeries, UltraTax CS, CCH Axcess, TaxSlayer Pro, ATX, TaxWise.
4. Document/practice platforms: SurePrep, TaxCaddy, SafeSend, Canopy, TaxDome, Liscio, Karbon, IRSLogics, PitBullTax.
5. AI and embedded-tax platforms: april, Column Tax, Instead, TaxGPT, Blue J, CoCounsel Tax, Filed, FlyFin, Keeper.

## Key lessons adapted into v0.1.4

- Document-first intake should exist before long form interviews.
- Uploaded documents should be classified by type and workflow.
- Every possible field-fill target needs a source and verification status.
- Staff/professional users need a return-order workpaper binder, not a pile of uploads.
- Clients need a "Done uploading" button so staff knows when to review.
- Tax-problem triage should remain the front door: notices, debt, back taxes, and anxiety first; returns second but live.
- OIC, penalty, hardship, and settlement workflows must be framed as screening/review, not promises.
- AI should classify, summarize, and draft workpapers; it should not be the final unchecked preparer.
