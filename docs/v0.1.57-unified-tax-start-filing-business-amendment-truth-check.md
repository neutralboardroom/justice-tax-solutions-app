# v0.1.57 Unified Tax Start + Filing / Business / Amendment / Truth Check Build

## Objective

Use Roger's Tax Prompt as the build guide and make Justice Tax Solutions clearly about filing, fixing, reviewing, amending, and understanding taxes. The platform should no longer feel like it is only for tax debt or scary tax notices.

## Public paths elevated

1. File a Personal Tax Return.
2. File a Business Tax Return.
3. Review Past Returns / Amendment Check.
4. Upload a Tax Notice — Free Truth Check.
5. Fix a Tax Problem.
6. I am not sure.

## Core implementation

- Added `src/unifiedTaxStart.js` for the public path map, summary builders, staff queue preview, and readiness audit.
- Reworked the homepage hero and action cards around the six clear paths.
- Added dedicated pages for personal filing, business filing, past-return review/amendment screening, and Free Truth Check.
- Added aliases to reduce friction while avoiding too many competing workflows.
- Updated dashboard, staff workbench, pricing, FAQ, app summary rendering, version markers, and smoke tests.

## Free and paid boundaries

- Free Tax Problem Truth Check remains free.
- Initial amendment screening is a free starting point.
- Paid filing, business return preparation/review, amended return work, notice response, bookkeeping cleanup, tax resolution, and professional review are shown only as options after scope and price are explained.
- No surprise charges or hidden payment gates were added.

## Staff routing added

Staff queue preview now tracks selected path, personal/business/both, entity type, agency, year/period, amount/deadline, urgency, issue categories, uploaded-doc status, business-record status, recommended review role, callback, free Truth Check confirmation, new personal/business filing flags, prior-year filing/review flags, amendment flags, deadline warning, and professional review suggestion.

## Safety posture preserved

- Live sensitive taxpayer uploads remain blocked.
- Redacted/sample-only uploads require acknowledgment.
- Final official form output remains blocked.
- E-file, direct debit, agency submission, Refund Transfer, refund advance, and bank products remain future-only.
- No guaranteed tax savings, refund, penalty relief, payment plan, settlement, amended-return benefit, business tax savings, or agency outcome.
- No fake government, CPA firm, EA, attorney, law firm, fake OCR, fake API, fake secure storage, fake e-file, or fake professional-review claims.

## Test coverage

- `npm install`

- `npm audit --audit-level=moderate`
- local `/health`
- key page smoke tests
- new API smoke tests
- no-file intake
- blocked upload without document/sample acknowledgment
- redacted/sample-only acknowledged upload
- final ZIP extraction and repeat tests

## Remaining blockers

Broad launch still requires managed production database, private object storage, malware scanning/quarantine, production transactional email, live Stripe/webhooks if paid launch is enabled, verified professional roster and credential controls, WISP/security plan, retention/deletion and incident-response procedures, backup/restore testing, official form QA, e-file/EFIN path, and final owner approval.
