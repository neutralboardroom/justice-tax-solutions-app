Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.28 adds campaign-ready service pages for real users while preserving production security, official-form QA, professional review, payment/quote, and no-guarantee guardrails.

- New public landing pages for IRS notices, NYS notices, NYC notices, tax debt, back taxes, amended returns, gig workers, IRS Forms 9465/433-F/1040-X, live online help, professional review, and Spanish starting help

## Guardrails

- Pages must route into existing intake or consultation flows.
- Pages must not bypass sensitive-upload gates.
- Pages must not claim final official forms are ready before PDF capture, mapping, sample-fill QA, client verification, and required professional signoff.
- Pages must not guarantee refunds, tax reduction, penalty relief, payment plans, offer-in-compromise acceptance, audit results, or agency response timing.
- Pages must clearly state Justice Tax Solutions is independent and not IRS, NYS, NYC, or a law firm.
