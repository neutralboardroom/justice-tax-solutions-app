# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.2 Audit

## Purpose

v0.1.2 moves the platform from a referral/intake foundation toward a real tax-service operations system. The focus remains tax problems first, with tax returns included, but the build now adds professional-review structure before live payments or sensitive production document handling.

## Added in v0.1.2

- Professional role catalog: PTIN preparer, accountant/bookkeeper, CPA, EA, and tax attorney escalation.
- Review tier catalog: starter organizer, notice action plan, return review, gig-worker review, debt-resolution review, and escalation review.
- Per-case review plan generated from the tax concern, documents, pathway, risk flags, and missing information.
- Readiness score for human/professional review.
- Required-document checklist by issue type.
- Client consent text for AI-organized but human-reviewed tax help.
- Paid-review request placeholder before Stripe is wired.
- Staff review queue endpoint.
- Staff professional registry endpoint.
- Staff case assignment to a professional.
- Staff review-note endpoint.
- Staff release-to-client endpoint.
- Client release-summary endpoint.
- Document download access logging.
- Access-log admin endpoint.
- Production-readiness endpoint and health-check summary.
- Expanded storage schema for review requests, professionals, release events, review notes, artifacts, and access logs.
- Updated staff and client dashboard behavior for review requests and release summaries.

## Production gaps intentionally still open

v0.1.2 is still not final production for live sensitive tax-document use. Before launch, add:

- Managed PostgreSQL persistence.
- Secure object storage with encryption and retention controls.
- Malware scanning for uploads.
- Staff/professional role permissions beyond the starter role model.
- MFA for staff/professional access.
- Password reset and email verification.
- Stripe checkout and webhook-confirmed payments.
- Real payment/release gate enforcement.
- PTIN/preparer signature capture and preparer workflow.
- New York tax preparer/facilitator registration compliance review.
- EFIN/e-file provider or e-file partner strategy.
- Client authorization, e-signature, and e-file consent workflows.
- Professional malpractice/E&O and engagement-letter review.

## Why this matters

The uploaded Immigration Oasis reference showed that platform maturity comes from more than forms. Its useful lessons were routing, referral attribution, dashboards, health checks, compliance copy, staff lanes, and deployment discipline. v0.1.2 adapts those lessons to tax by adding the missing tax-service layer: who reviews, what documents are required, what can be released, and what must remain blocked until professional/payment controls exist.
