# v0.1.71 — IRS Individual Income Tax Form Engine

Adds 27 official IRS PDFs covering Form 1040, Form 1040-SR, common schedules, Form 1040-X, Form 2441, Form 8863, and Form 1098-T. The build records checksums, inventories AcroForm fields, generates safe samples, performs automated page-render checks, and exposes guided form-recommendation and staff evidence APIs. Human semantic verification, human page-by-page QA, professional approval, owner approval, live sensitive uploads, and government submission remain blocked.
