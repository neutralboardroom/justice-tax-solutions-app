# v0.1.53 IRS Authorization Bundle + Refund Bank-Product Readiness Gate

This build adds the next controlled form bundle after Form 433-F: IRS Forms 2848 and 8821.

## Why this bundle was chosen

Justice Tax Solutions is tax-problem-first. Tax notices, tax debt, unfiled returns, installment agreements, transcript review, and professional review often require a safe way to decide whether a taxpayer needs:

- Form 2848, Power of Attorney and Declaration of Representative, when an eligible representative may act before the IRS; or
- Form 8821, Tax Information Authorization, when someone only needs authority to inspect or receive confidential tax information.

These forms are related, high-impact, and risk-sensitive. They are a good next bundle because they strengthen the staff/professional workflow without pretending final IRS submission is ready.

## What remains blocked

- Final official IRS Form 2848 output.
- Final official IRS Form 8821 output.
- Full TIN/PTIN/signature handling in public mode.
- Taxpayer or representative electronic signature workflow.
- IRS Tax Pro Account submission.
- CAF submission, fax, mail, or agency submission claims.
- Professional representation claims unless a qualified representative is verified and assigned.
- Refund Transfer, refund advance, early refund access, debit-card loading, and pay-by-refund claims until e-file, vendor, disclosure, security, and operations gates pass.
