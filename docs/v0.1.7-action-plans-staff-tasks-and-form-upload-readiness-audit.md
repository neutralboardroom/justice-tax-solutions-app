# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.7 Audit

## Focus
v0.1.7 continues the platform build while official IRS/NYS/NYC form ZIP uploads are deferred until the next session. The goal is to make the app more operationally useful before forms arrive.

## Added
- Tax-problem playbook engine in `src/workflowEngine.js`.
- Case action-plan endpoint: `GET /api/cases/:id/action-plan`.
- Client/staff messaging endpoint: `POST /api/cases/:id/messages`.
- Public playbook catalog endpoint: `GET /api/tax/problem-playbooks`.
- Built-in official form upload checklist endpoint: `GET /api/tax/forms-upload-tomorrow-checklist`.
- Staff task board endpoint: `GET /api/staff/task-board`.
- Manual staff task endpoint: `POST /api/staff/cases/:id/task`.
- Staff task status endpoint: `POST /api/staff/tasks/:id/status`.
- New pages: `/forms-upload-checklist.html` and `/staff-tasks.html`.
- Case action plans are generated at intake and can be regenerated from the case endpoint.
- PostgreSQL schema placeholders for tasks, client checklist events, form upload sessions, and staff notifications.

## Playbooks included
- IRS notice triage.
- Tax debt / cannot pay.
- Unfiled or prior-year returns.
- Self-employed / gig worker taxes.
- New York State / NYC tax issue.
- Tax return preparation/review.

## Safety / compliance principle
The platform now makes the operating rule explicit: action plans, extracted fields, and uploaded forms are not enough to file, sign, respond to agencies, or make tax-debt promises. Client-facing tax output still requires source verification, human/professional review, payment/client approval gates where applicable, and professional compliance.

## Official-form upload readiness for tomorrow
The new upload checklist asks for smaller, focused ZIPs, preferably one agency per ZIP; official filenames; instructions included where available; source URLs; and tax-year folders if mixed. Forms will remain catalog/mapping assets until source verification, field extraction, mapping, sample-fill QA, calculation checks, and professional review are completed.

## Still not production-ready for live sensitive tax documents
Before production use, configure managed PostgreSQL, private object storage, malware scanning, MFA/staff approval, real email delivery, live Stripe webhooks, professional credential procedures, OCR/document-AI provider, official form mapping QA, and e-file/EFIN or partner workflow.
