# v0.1.29 Staff Cockpit + Analytics Audit

Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.29 adds a privacy-safe staff cockpit and marketing analytics layer for private pilot and early marketing operations.

## Added

- Staff cockpit endpoint aggregating cases, quotes, payments, professional sessions, appointment scheduling, referrals, rewards, professionals, documents, messages, and operational alerts.
- Conversion funnel endpoint tracking case creation, quote request, quote sent, quote approval, payment request, paid, professional session request, appointment scheduled, and release.
- Marketing attribution endpoint for source/campaign/referral/pathway reporting.
- Operational alerts endpoint for high-risk cases, waiting quotes, approved quotes needing payment request, appointments needing scheduling, queued messages, professional verification gaps, and live-sensitive document warnings.
- Public privacy-safe analytics event taxonomy and sanitized analytics event recording endpoint.
- New `/staff-cockpit.html` page.
- Intake now captures UTM/source/landing/conversion fields where present.

## Guardrails

- Analytics events must not include SSNs, bank/account details, notice text, document content, tax balances, passwords, tokens, or private tax facts.
- Cockpit is operational only and does not replace professional review, release gates, client verification, official-form QA, payment gates, or production security gates.
- Broad paid marketing should wait until cockpit tracking is reviewed with real pilot traffic.

## Remaining work

- Real email provider delivery and daily staff digest.
- Live Stripe webhook validation in production.
- Calendar/provider API sync when Google/Calendly/Microsoft/Zoom credentials are configured.
- Optional privacy/cookie banner if third-party pixels are added later.
