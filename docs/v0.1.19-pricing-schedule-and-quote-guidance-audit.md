# Justice Tax Solutions v0.1.20 Pricing Schedule + Quote Guidance Audit

## Purpose
v0.1.20 adds a structured pricing catalog for AI-first form completion, notice help, return workflows, tax debt, referral rewards, and professional escalation.

## Pricing philosophy
- Start free so anxious taxpayers can understand where to begin.
- Price low-risk AI-assisted organizers/drafts separately from human/professional review.
- Minimize human work by making AI ask the full interview, request missing information, validate answers, and prepare field candidates.
- Escalate only exceptions, high-risk facts, complex tax positions, professional signoff, or legal-advice-sensitive matters.
- Never imply guaranteed refunds, guaranteed tax relief, pennies-on-the-dollar outcomes, or guaranteed agency acceptance.

## New implementation
- `src/pricingCatalog.js` centralizes public pricing tiers, form pricing targets, add-ons, pricing rules, quote estimates, and staff pricing guidance.
- `/api/platform/pricing-schedule` exposes the full structured schedule.
- `/api/platform/form-pricing-matrix` exposes first-wave form pricing targets.
- `/api/platform/service-price-estimate` estimates a public quote from product/form/service/add-ons.
- `/api/staff/pricing-guide` gives staff/admin quote guidance and upgrade rules.
- `/pricing.html` now shows service levels, form-by-form pricing targets, add-ons, custom quote triggers, and compliance disclaimers.

## Important gates
The form prices are targets for verified, print-ready workflows. No official form should be released as client-ready until the PDF has been officially captured, field-mapped, sample-filled, checked for overflow/signatures/calculations, and verified.

## Regression focus
Keep referral rewards aligned with pricing. Keep Stripe product keys stable. Preserve old payment/referral behavior while expanding the public schedule.
