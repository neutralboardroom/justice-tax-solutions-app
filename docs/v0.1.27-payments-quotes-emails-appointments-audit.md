# v0.1.27 Payments, Quotes, Emails, and Appointment Confirmations Audit

## Purpose

v0.1.27 hardens the operational layer needed before real paid users: price quote approval, Stripe/payment request alignment, no-sensitive-details transactional messages, appointment confirmations, reminders, reschedule/cancel tracking, and staff payment operations visibility.

## Added

- `src/paymentWorkflow.js`
- Quote records and customer approval workflow
- Staff-created quotes and quote status updates
- Payment request creation from approved quotes
- Stripe checkout support for approved quote amounts
- Safe transactional message template library
- No-sensitive-details email policy
- Appointment confirmation/reminder/reschedule/cancel message records
- Staff payment operations board
- Case payment summary and customer quote request endpoints

## Guardrails

- Payment does not mean a form is ready to file, sign, submit, or release.
- Quote approval is required before quote-based payment requests.
- Transactional messages avoid sensitive taxpayer facts in subjects and bodies.
- Stripe checkout can use approved quote amounts, but real payment readiness still requires live Stripe keys and tested webhook handling.
- Appointment confirmations and reminders are manual-first until a real email/calendar provider is configured and tested.

## Remaining blockers

- Real email provider/domain authentication is not configured in code by default.
- Live Stripe webhook must be tested in production before paid release is trusted.
- Automated reminders require a future scheduled job/worker or external email automation.
- Calendar APIs remain planned; staff can still use manual meeting links and existing Calendly/Google/Teams/Zoom links.
