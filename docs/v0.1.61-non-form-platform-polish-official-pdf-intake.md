# v0.1.61 Non-form platform polish + official-PDF intake infrastructure

## Purpose

v0.1.61 implements Roger's decision to stop adding additional IRS form bundles until official PDFs are uploaded or captured. The build preserves existing organizers and readiness work, then improves platform polish, customer flow, staff dashboards, pricing/help language, Spanish parity, marketing pages, upload safety, deployment readiness, and official-PDF intake infrastructure.

## Key principle

Logical organizers are not the same as paid-user-ready official form output. A final official form requires official PDF capture, checksum/source tracking, field/coordinate mapping, sample-filled PDF generation, visual QA, client verification, professional/staff release, production security approval, and owner/staff release.
