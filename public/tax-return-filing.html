Personal filing intake

Need to file a new personal return? Start here.

We help organize current-year and prior-year personal tax returns, including W-2, 1099, self-employed/gig-worker, Schedule C, dependents, education credits, child-related credits, filing-status, estimated-tax, NYS/NYC, rejected-return, and review-before-filing questions.

What this start flow asks

  • What tax year do you need to file?
  • Is this current-year or prior-year?
  • Have you filed this year already?
  • Do you have W-2, 1099, self-employment, gig-work, unemployment, or investment income?
  • Do you have dependents, education expenses, child-related credits, or filing-status questions?
  • Do you need NYS/NYC return help or review before filing?

What the output can include

  • Personal Filing Readiness Summary.
  • Return type and tax year/period.
  • Filing-readiness checklist.
  • Documents needed.
  • Possible review level.
  • Whether PTIN/preparer, CPA, EA, or accountant review may be needed.
  • Next step to prepare, file, or review.

This is an intake and readiness flow. It does not guarantee refund amount, tax savings, filing result, or IRS/state/city acceptance. Paid preparation or filing support begins only if options are explained and you choose deeper help.
