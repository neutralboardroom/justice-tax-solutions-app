Review Past Returns for Possible Missed Savings

Many people file and move on — but prior returns may contain missed credits, overlooked deductions, incorrect income reporting, business expense issues, filing-status problems, or other items worth reviewing. Justice Tax Solutions can review recent filed returns and help you understand whether an amended return may be worth considering.

If an amendment may be appropriate, we explain your options before you decide. If amending may help and the service is available for your situation, we can help prepare and file the amended return if you choose paid help.

Personal returns we can screen

  • Recent filed federal and state personal returns.
  • W-2, 1099, self-employed, Schedule C, or gig-worker issues.
  • Possible missed deductions, credits, dependents, education credits, child-related credits, or filing-status questions.
  • Possible income-reporting problems, refund holds, or notices tied to a filed return.
  • NYS/NYC or other state/local questions where applicable.

Business returns we can screen

  • Prior business returns or Schedule C returns where appropriate.
  • Possible missed business expenses, contractor/1099, vehicle, home office, depreciation, or asset questions.
  • LLC, partnership, S corp, or corporation issues routed for professional review when needed.
  • Payroll, sales-tax, entity, or bookkeeping concerns that may affect whether amending is worth reviewing.
  • Business tax notices connected to a previously filed return.

What the screening can produce

  • Years selected for review.
  • Personal/business/both classification.
  • Possible amendment opportunity: yes, no, or unknown.
  • Possible issue categories.
  • Documents still needed.
  • Federal, state, city, or business amendment questions.
  • Professional review recommendation where needed.
  • Paid amendment preparation option only if applicable.

Pricing and deadline notes

Your initial past-return review starts with a free screening. If an amended return may be worth preparing, we explain the paid options before you choose.

Refund and amendment deadlines can matter. For federal refund claims, IRS guidance generally says Form 1040-X must be filed within 3 years after the original return was filed or 2 years after the tax was paid, whichever is later, but deadlines and exceptions should be verified before action. State and city rules may differ.

No guarantees, no pressure

Amending a return is not always beneficial or appropriate. A tax professional should review before filing an amended return. IRS, NYS, NYC, or other taxing authorities make final decisions. Justice Tax Solutions does not guarantee a refund, savings, amended-return acceptance, business tax savings, or any government outcome.
