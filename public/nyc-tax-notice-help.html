Likely routes

Questions we may ask

  • Is the notice about UBT, property, business tax, parking, or another NYC issue?
  • Are you self-employed or a partnership?

When we escalate

AI should ask follow-up questions first, then route to the lowest safe review level. A customer can also request a professional simply for reassurance.

  • NYC UBT
