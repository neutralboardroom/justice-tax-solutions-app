v0.1.56 controlled IRS appeals and abatement bundle

Organize penalty abatement, refund, and collection appeal forms before review.

This workspace covers IRS Form 843, Form 9423, and Form 12153 in controlled organizer mode. Users can organize facts, deadlines, form fit, and supporting-document summaries without creating final IRS output or submitting anything to the IRS.

Do not enter full SSNs, ITINs, EINs, bank/account numbers, signatures, exact private financial details, or unredacted notices here yet. Use last-4 identifiers, approximate amounts, and redacted summaries only until production sensitive-data controls are approved.

Form 843

Claim for Refund and Request for Abatement. Useful for certain penalty abatement, interest abatement, and refund/abatement claims when another form is not required.

  • Checks claim reason and period.
  • Flags Form 843 fit risks.
  • Computations/support still require review.

Form 9423

Collection Appeal Request. Useful for CAP issues such as lien, levy, seizure, or installment-agreement rejection/modification/termination.

  • Deadline and routing can be urgent.
  • Manager conference/timing must be checked.
  • Representation may require Form 2848.

Form 12153

Collection Due Process or Equivalent Hearing. Useful when a CDP lien/levy notice offers appeal rights.

  • Notice date/deadline must be verified.
  • Collection alternatives may need financial forms.
  • High-risk issues require professional review.
