Clear pricing

Choose AI completion, PTIN review, EA review, CPA review, or tax attorney review.

Justice Tax Solutions should keep human work to the minimum safe level. The platform asks the questions, follows up when facts change, prepares field candidates, and routes the case to the review level actually needed. A customer can also request a professional review for reassurance, confidence, or a second look even when the AI-only path might otherwise be available.

Upgrade rule

The platform should start with the lowest safe level. If answers reveal a risk that requires PTIN, EA, CPA, or tax attorney review, the client should see the reason, receive credit for prior eligible platform payments when appropriate, and approve the upgrade before the higher-tier fee is charged.

Taxes, penalties, interest, government payments, filing charges, transcript fees, refunds, chargebacks, and separate third-party attorney/professional fees are separate. No refund amount, tax reduction, payment plan, penalty relief, offer-in-compromise, audit result, or agency outcome is guaranteed.
