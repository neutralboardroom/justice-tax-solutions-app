Credits and dependents help

Credits and dependents tax form help.

Organize information for five common IRS forms involving dependents, earned income credit, credit reinstatement, preparer due diligence, and retirement-savings credit. Eligibility, signatures, certifications, and filing decisions require human review.

Schedule EIC

Collects and maps qualifying-child information after earned income credit eligibility and the credit amount are determined separately. It does not calculate or promise EIC.

Sample form previews use fictional data and are not taxpayer filing documents.

Form 8880

Calculates a narrow 2025 saver’s-credit lane from verified contribution, deferral, distribution, AGI, filing-status, and Credit Limit Worksheet information.

The first lane does not calculate the Credit Limit Worksheet and stops on unsupported age, student, dependent, or foreign-income facts.

Form 8862

Professional-review intake for credits that were previously reduced or disallowed. Completion of the form does not establish that the taxpayer may claim the credit again.

Form 8332

Signature-sensitive organizer for one child’s release or revocation. The platform does not decide custody or automatically insert the custodial parent’s signature.

Form 8867

A paid-preparer due-diligence questionnaire. AI may organize questions and records, but the responsible preparer must personally review and certify the checklist.

Official-source controls

All five forms and the Form 8862 and Form 8867 instructions are checksum-tracked, technically inventoried, and tied to official IRS source URLs and revisions.

What the AI helper may do

  • Ask relevant questions one step at a time and explain why each answer is needed.
  • Build document and missing-information checklists.
  • Organize reviewed answers for the appropriate form sections.
  • Flag prior disallowance, joint-return, residency, tiebreaker, student, dependent, age, and signature issues for review.
  • Stop rather than guess when facts are incomplete or outside the supported lane.
Review is required: A generated organizer or preview is not signed, filed, e-filed, accepted, or approved by the IRS. Eligibility, calculations, supporting records, signatures, and preparer responsibilities must be checked before filing.
