Launch rule

Justice Tax Solutions can be deployed to Render for testing now. It should not be treated as live production for sensitive taxpayer documents until managed database, private storage, malware scanning, live Stripe webhooks, staff approval, professional verification, and secure email/recovery are configured.

When live mode is eventually enabled, AI remains an organizer. Human/professional review remains required before filing, signing, agency responses, payment-plan/OIC advice, or release of final review output.
