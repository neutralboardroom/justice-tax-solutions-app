v0.1.62 full-platform audit

Polish the whole platform without weakening the safety gates.

This page keeps the next practical work focused: customer flow, staff dashboards, pricing/help language, Spanish parity, marketing safety, upload safety, deployment readiness, and official-PDF intake readiness. It does not add new IRS forms.

Customer path

Users should be able to start with a personal return, business return, past-return review, Free Truth Check, tax problem, or “not sure” without learning internal form numbers.

Staff path

Staff should classify the case, protect sensitive data, route the right review level, and keep final output blocked until the proper gate is complete.

Owner path

Owner readiness should focus on domain/SSL, environment variables, official PDF ingestion, professional roster, payment/email/calendar configuration, and deployment smoke tests.

No new logical-only forms

Official PDFs are now the next form bottleneck.

The platform should not keep adding more IRS form organizers without exact official PDFs. Paid-user-ready form output requires official PDF capture, checksum/source record, fields or coordinates locked, sample-filled PDF, visual QA, case-value verification, staff/professional release, production security approval, and owner approval.

Public copy quality gate

  • Clear free starting point.
  • No surprise payment gate before the basic summary.
  • No refund, savings, settlement, payment-plan, penalty, or amended-return guarantees.
  • No live e-file, agency submission, Refund Transfer, or refund advance claims.
  • No request for unredacted sensitive taxpayer documents in controlled mode.

Staff daily quality gate

  • Classify the selected path first.
  • Check urgency and deadline signals.
  • Confirm safe document posture.
  • Route CPA/EA/PTIN/accountant/bookkeeper/payroll/sales-tax/tax attorney review honestly.
  • Keep emails, notes, and analytics free of sensitive tax details.

Spanish parity

Spanish touchpoints should explain the same value and safety: presentar declaración personal, declaración de negocio, revisar declaraciones pasadas, revisión gratuita inicial, avisos, problemas de impuestos, and no guarantees.

Marketing safety

Marketing can be strong, but it must stay honest: file, fix, review, amend, and understand taxes; start with a free Truth Check; review past returns carefully; no fake settlement, savings, refund, e-file, or bank-product claims.
