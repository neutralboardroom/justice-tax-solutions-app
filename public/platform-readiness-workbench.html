v0.1.62 readiness workbench

Polish the platform while official PDFs are being gathered.

This workbench keeps the project moving without wasting time on more logical-only IRS form bundles. It focuses on customer flow, staff dashboards, pricing/help clarity, Spanish parity, marketing language, upload safety, deployment readiness, and official-PDF intake infrastructure.

Customer flow

Keep the five front doors clear: personal filing, business filing, past-return review, free Truth Check, and tax-problem help. The “not sure” path remains available for anxious users.

Staff dashboard

Staff lanes should classify the selected path, check urgency, protect sensitive data, route professional review, and block final official output until PDF QA is complete.

Pricing/help

Free Truth Check and initial amendment screening remain clear. Paid filing, amendment preparation, business work, or professional review is explained before the user chooses deeper help.

Spanish parity

Spanish pages should explain the same safe start, no guarantees, free-vs-paid boundary, prior-return review, business help, and document-safety limits.

Marketing

Lead with filing, business taxes, second-look prior-return review, and free next-step summaries. Avoid “settle for pennies,” guaranteed refund, or guaranteed amendment language.

Official PDFs

New form buildout resumes only after official PDFs are uploaded or captured, checksummed, mapped, sample-filled, visually QA’d, and released.

Current build policy

No more form bundles without official PDFs.

Existing organizers are preserved, but the next form work should use the actual official IRS/NYS/NYC PDFs so the platform can progress toward real paid-user-ready official output instead of more planning-only bundles.
