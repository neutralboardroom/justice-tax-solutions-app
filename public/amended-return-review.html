Major feature

Review past returns and check if an amendment may help.

Already filed? We can review recent personal or business returns to see whether changes may be available that could help and whether an amended return may be worth considering.

Common review areas

  • Missed deductions, credits, dependents, filing status, education credits, child-related credits, and EITC questions where relevant.
  • 1099/self-employment income and expense issues, W-2 or withholding problems, incorrect income reporting, missing forms, and Schedule C questions.
  • Business expense, LLC, partnership, S corp, corporation, depreciation, asset, vehicle, home office, payroll, sales-tax, and state/local issues that need routing.
  • Prior preparer/accountant concerns and notices connected to a previously filed return.

What the summary can include

  • Years selected or uploaded.
  • Personal/business return type.
  • Possible amendment opportunity: yes/no/unknown.
  • Possible issue categories and documents still needed.
  • Federal, state, or business amendment question.
  • Professional review suggestion.
  • Paid amendment preparation option if applicable.

Important amendment deadline note

For federal refund claims, amendment/refund deadlines can matter. IRS Form 1040-X instructions generally refer to filing within 3 years after the original return was filed or 2 years after the tax was paid, whichever is later, but deadlines and exceptions should be verified before action.

An amended return may be worth reviewing, but it is not guaranteed to help or produce a refund. IRS, NYS, NYC, and other taxing authorities make final decisions. Professional review may be needed before filing.
