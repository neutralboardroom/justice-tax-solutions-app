People who received an IRS CP notice, balance due letter, proposed change, missing return notice, penalty notice, or confusing account letter.

Likely routes

Questions we may ask

  • What notice number is shown?
  • What deadline is listed?
  • Does the notice show a balance due, proposed change, missing return, or penalty?
  • Did you already respond or call the agency?

When we escalate

AI should ask follow-up questions first, then route to the lowest safe review level. A customer can also request a professional simply for reassurance.

  • notice deadline within 14 days
  • audit/exam language
  • appeals language
  • fraud/criminal language
  • large balance
  • prior unfiled years
  • business/payroll facts
