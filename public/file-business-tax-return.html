Business taxes? Start here.

File a business return or organize your business tax problem. For LLCs, self-employed workers, contractors, partnerships, S corps, corporations, and small businesses.

What this can help organize

  • Schedule C sole proprietor and single-member LLC questions.
  • Partnership, S corporation, C corporation, and multi-member LLC routing.
  • Business income and expense organizer.
  • Bookkeeping cleanup before filing.
  • Profit and loss, balance sheet, assets, vehicles, home office, and inventory questions where relevant.
  • Payroll tax, sales-tax, contractor/1099, W-9, and 1099-NEC issues.
  • NYS/NYC business tax issues and business tax notices.

What the flow asks

  • What type of business do you have?
  • What tax year or period needs help?
  • Is this a new return, prior-year return, amended return, notice response, or cleanup issue?
  • Is bookkeeping complete? Do you have a profit and loss statement?
  • Do you have payroll, sales tax, contractors, assets, vehicles, inventory, or home-office expenses?
  • Do you need CPA, EA, accountant, bookkeeper, payroll, or sales-tax specialist review?

Complex business returns, payroll returns, sales-tax returns, amended business returns, and high-risk business tax notices require appropriate professional review and operational readiness. This page uses intake/routing language and does not promise final filing or government acceptance.
