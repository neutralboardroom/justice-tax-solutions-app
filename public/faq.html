FAQ

Are you the IRS, New York State, or NYC?

No. Justice Tax Solutions is independent and is not a government agency.

Are you a law firm?

No. Legal advice is provided only by a licensed attorney if attorney review is separately arranged.

Can you prepare tax returns?

The platform supports return organization, preparation workflow, and human review. Paid federal return preparation must involve a valid PTIN preparer where required.

Can you reduce my tax debt?

No result is guaranteed. The platform can screen for payment plans, hardship, penalty relief, or offer-in-compromise options and route cases to qualified professionals.

Does the referral partner see my taxes?

No. Referral tools track attribution, scans, starts, cases, and reward status. Private tax details should not be shared with partners unless the user separately authorizes it.

Does the app file automatically?

No. Users and qualified professionals must review before filing, signing, or responding to an agency.
