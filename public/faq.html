FAQ

Common questions about filing, fixing, reviewing, and amending taxes.

Justice Tax Solutions helps users start with a return, business records, a past return, a tax notice, or a tax problem. This FAQ keeps the free start, paid options, professional-review boundaries, document-safety rules, and no-guarantee language clear.

Can Justice Tax Solutions help me file a new personal tax return?

Yes. The platform supports a personal return filing intake for current-year and prior-year personal returns, including W-2, 1099/self-employed, gig-worker, dependents, credits, and review-before-filing questions where appropriate.

Can Justice Tax Solutions help me file a business tax return?

Yes. Business tax intake is a main path. It can help organize Schedule C, sole proprietor, single-member LLC, partnership, S corp, C corp, business records, bookkeeping cleanup, payroll/sales-tax routing, and professional review needs where appropriate.

What is the Free Tax Problem Truth Check?

It is a free plain-language starting summary for a tax notice or tax problem. It can help identify the likely agency, issue category, year/period, urgency signals, documents to gather, mistakes to avoid, and possible next step.

Is the first Truth Check really free?

Yes. The basic Tax Problem Truth Check Summary should not have a payment gate. Paid filing, response preparation, amendment preparation, business tax work, bookkeeping cleanup, or professional review is offered only if you choose deeper help after scope and pricing are explained.

Can you review my past tax returns?

Yes. Recent filed personal and business returns can be reviewed to see whether missed deductions, credits, business expenses, income-reporting issues, filing-status concerns, or other items may make an amended return worth considering.

Can you help file an amended return?

If review shows that an amended return may be appropriate and the service is available for your situation, Justice Tax Solutions can explain the next steps and paid preparation/review options before you decide.

Is an amended return guaranteed to get me money back?

No. Amended returns do not always produce a refund or savings, and sometimes amending may not be the right step. IRS, NYS, NYC, or other taxing authorities make final decisions.

What if I have a deadline?

Do not ignore a time-sensitive notice. Start with the deadline printed on the notice and verify it against the original document and official sources. Urgent collection, court, criminal, trust-fund, payroll, or sales-tax issues may need professional review before action.

Can you help with payroll or sales-tax issues?

The platform can intake and route payroll or sales-tax concerns carefully, but these issues may require specialized CPA, EA, accountant, bookkeeper, payroll specialist, sales-tax specialist, or tax attorney review.

Are you the IRS, New York State, or NYC?

No. Justice Tax Solutions is independent and is not the IRS, NYS Department of Taxation and Finance, NYC Department of Finance, or any government agency.

Are you a law firm or CPA firm?

No, not unless a verified professional or firm relationship is separately confirmed and approved. Legal advice is provided only by a licensed attorney if attorney review is separately arranged.

Does the app file automatically?

No. Final filing, e-file, agency submission, direct debit, Refund Transfer, refund advances, and official form output are not active unless later configured, tested, professionally reviewed where required, and approved.

Can you guarantee a settlement, penalty removal, payment plan, refund, or business tax savings?

No. The platform can help organize information and route review, but it does not guarantee settlement, penalty removal, offer-in-compromise acceptance, audit result, payment-plan approval, refund, amended-return benefit, or business tax savings.

Is my uploaded tax or business document safe?

During the current controlled launch, live sensitive uploads remain blocked. Use no-file or redacted/sample-only mode and do not upload originals, full SSNs, account numbers, full tax returns, transcripts, unredacted notices, payroll records, or bank data.

Can you review business returns too?

Business owners can start with Schedule C, LLC, partnership, S corp, corporation, business expense, depreciation, vehicle, home office, payroll, sales-tax, or state/local issues where appropriate. Complex business issues may need professional review.

Is the first amendment screening free?

The first screening can be a free starting point. Amended-return preparation, professional review, or filing support may be paid only if you choose deeper help after pricing is explained.

Should I amend just because I might get money back?

Not automatically. Amending can have deadlines and consequences. A professional should review whether amending is appropriate before filing, and no refund, savings, acceptance, or outcome is guaranteed.
