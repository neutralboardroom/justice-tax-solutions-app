What this guide does

It asks plain-English questions, identifies forms and schedules that may apply, organizes the information needed, and routes complex or amended returns for professional review.

Simple 2025 Form 1040 W-2 starting path

For a simple 2025 calendar-year return, the starting path can organize W-2 wages, Single or Married Filing Jointly status, the basic standard deduction, and taxable income below $100,000 when there are no dependents, credits, other income, itemized deductions, or other complications.

The AI helper asks relevant questions, explains why information is needed, performs supported calculations, and stops when the return appears to need a different or more advanced path.

Before filing: Calculations, form details, supporting records, and taxpayer information must be verified. Complex or unsupported facts require the appropriate professional review.
