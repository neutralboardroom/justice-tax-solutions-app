Free first step

Upload a tax notice or describe the problem for a Free Truth Check.

Do not panic, but do not ignore tax notices. Start with a free plain-language Tax Problem Truth Check Summary so you can understand what the issue appears to be and what may need attention.

Supported issue types

  • IRS, NYS, NYC Department of Finance, other state/city, business, payroll, or sales-tax notice.
  • Balance due, penalty, CP2000, missing return, refund hold, identity verification, audit/exam letter, levy, lien, garnishment, warrant, or collection notice.
  • Payment plan, offer-in-compromise, currently-not-collectible, penalty abatement, prior-year filing, amended-return, 1099/W-2, estimated-tax, and “I am not sure what this is” issues.

Your free summary can include

  • What this appears to be.
  • Which agency appears involved.
  • Personal or business issue if known.
  • Tax year/period, notice/form number, amount, and deadline if provided.
  • Why it matters and what could happen if ignored, phrased carefully.
  • Documents to gather, mistakes to avoid, and questions to ask.
  • Whether filing, business filing, prior-year review, amendment review, or professional review may be relevant.

The basic Truth Check Summary is free. It is not a final tax determination, legal advice, representation, or a guarantee. Deadlines and options should be verified against the original notice and official sources.
