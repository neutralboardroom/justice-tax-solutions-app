Deployment readiness

Deploy only after the ZIP, version, safety, and live smoke checks are clean.

This page is a practical v0.1.62 deployment closeout checklist. It does not mean the platform is ready for live sensitive taxpayer uploads, final official forms, e-file, agency submission, payments, or refund bank products.

Before deploy

  • Confirm clean ZIP extracts.
  • Run npm install, npm run check, and npm audit --audit-level=moderate.
  • Confirm package, README, docs, health, and smoke tests show v0.1.62.
  • Confirm no .env, node_modules, data, storage uploads, logs, runtime database, captured PDFs, or secrets are in the ZIP.
  • Confirm live sensitive-upload flags remain false unless Roger explicitly approves after security gates.

After deploy

  • Check live /health version.
  • Smoke-test home, pricing, dashboard, staff, FAQ, Spanish, official forms, official PDF intake, document safety, and deployment pages.
  • Confirm domain/SSL and PUBLIC_BASE_URL.
  • Confirm Stripe/email/calendar/EFIN/refund bank products are not claimed live unless truly configured and tested.
  • Confirm public users still see no-file or redacted/sample-only upload guidance.

Go / no-go summary

Public site viewing

Go after deployment smoke test.

Controlled first users

Go only for no-sensitive or redacted/sample workflows with clear limits.

Paid final form output

Blocked until official PDF QA, operations, security, and release gates pass.

Post-deployment experience smoke test

Confirm the live site still feels simple and safe.

  • Homepage shows the six main paths clearly.
  • Prior-return review remains prominent.
  • Pricing explains free starting points and paid options without surprise gates.
  • Spanish entry points remain visible and careful.
  • Official PDF intake is staff/admin infrastructure, not a public sensitive-document promise.
  • Live sensitive uploads, final official forms, e-file, agency submission, Refund Transfer, and refund advance remain blocked.

v0.1.64 release continuity gate

Do not announce a deployment until data continuity is verified.

After every release, confirm existing customer accounts, login records, saved drafts, case progress, uploaded document references, quotes, payments, staff notes, accountant/CPA/professional work, and release gates are still present. If any continuity check fails, pause user invitations and restore from backup before continuing.
