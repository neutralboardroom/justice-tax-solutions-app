Official-PDF intake infrastructure

Pause new form buildout until the official PDFs are in the mapping queue.

This is the new source-first workflow for turning IRS/NYS/NYC forms into paid-user-ready output. Organizers and logical maps are useful, but final official forms require the exact official PDF, checksum, field/coordinate lock, sample-filled PDFs, visual QA, client verification, staff/professional release, and production security approval.

Current rule: no more new logical-only IRS form bundles. Use this page to upload or capture official PDFs, then map and QA them one at a time before any paid-user-ready final output claim.

Readiness ladder

  1. Official PDF uploaded or captured.
  2. Official source, edition, and checksum recorded.
  3. Fillable PDF fields or overlay coordinates locked.
  4. Sample-filled PDFs generated.
  5. Visual QA checks placement, overflow, checkboxes, dates, blanks, signature/preparer areas, and page fit.
  6. Client values verified for each paid case.
  7. Professional/staff release recorded where required.
  8. Production security and owner release approved.

What stays blocked

  • Final official IRS/NYS/NYC output.
  • E-file or agency submission.
  • Full SSNs, EINs, bank data, IP PINs, signatures, or direct debit in public mode.
  • Refund Transfer, refund advance, bank products, or direct debit.
  • Any guaranteed refund, savings, amendment result, payment plan, penalty removal, or agency acceptance claim.

Official-form readiness is not the same as taxpayer upload readiness.

Official PDFs are public government source materials used for mapping and QA. Taxpayer returns, notices, W-2s, 1099s, transcripts, payroll records, SSNs, EINs, and bank data are sensitive client information and remain blocked in live public mode until production security gates are approved.

Official PDF readiness ladder

Official PDF intake is only the first step.

  1. Official PDF uploaded or captured.
  2. Source, edition, and checksum recorded.
  3. Fields or overlay coordinates locked.
  4. Sample-filled PDF generated.
  5. Visual QA passed for placement, overflow, checkboxes, dates, signature/preparer blanks, and sensitive fields.
  6. Client values verified and staff/professional release recorded.
  7. Production security and owner approval completed before any paid-user final output.

v0.1.64 official-PDF continuity

Official PDF source records and mapping progress must be preserved.

When Roger uploads official IRS PDFs, their source URL, checksum, field/coordinate map, sample-filled output, visual QA, staff/professional release status, and owner approval must survive every deployment. Do not store official mapping progress only in a replaceable source folder.

Official-PDF intake is an admin/staff mapping workflow, not a customer filing promise.
