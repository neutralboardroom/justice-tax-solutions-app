v0.1.56 launch readiness

What can be public now, and what must stay blocked.

Justice Tax Solutions can be reviewed publicly and used by invited first users only under no-file or redacted/sample-only rules. Full production still requires external security, professional, payment, domain, email, official-form, and owner-approval gates.

Reasonable to show publicly now

  • Public website and service pages
  • Free starting point intake without real sensitive documents
  • Tax notice next-step guidance
  • Taxpayer Action Center, review-level self-check, and urgency triage
  • Document Safety Center and Safe Summary Builder
  • Pricing/review-level explanation with clear limits

Still blocked before full launch

  • Unredacted taxpayer document uploads
  • Broad paid ads or unrestricted launch
  • Live Stripe charges without webhook/email/SOP testing
  • Final official IRS/NYS/NYC form output
  • E-file or agency filing claims
  • Guarantees about refunds, settlements, penalties, payment plans, audits, or agency decisions

Current safest launch mode

Public site review + invite-only no-sensitive first users.

Use no-file intake and redacted/sample documents only. Route complex, urgent, high-dollar, audit, collection, criminal, business payroll/sales-tax, or legal-risk matters to the appropriate professional review level. The urgency triage is a routing helper only, not an official deadline calculator or legal/tax advice.
