Staff priority lane

Prior-return review / amendment opportunity cases must not be treated as minor add-ons.

Classify clearly

  • New Past Return Review
  • Possible Amendment Opportunity
  • Waiting for Prior Returns
  • Business return / Schedule C review

Review flags

  • Missed deduction or credit
  • Filing-status/dependent issue
  • W-2/1099/income reporting
  • Business expense/depreciation/vehicle/home office
  • NYS/NYC/state/local amendment question

Safety rule

Do not promise money back, savings, IRS/state acceptance, or that amending is the right step. Flag refund-deadline/statute questions and route business, payroll, sales-tax, or high-risk cases for appropriate professional review.

Unified tax intake queue

Staff queue must separate filing, review/amendment, Truth Check, and tax-problem cases.

Public path

  • New Personal Filing Intake
  • New Business Filing Intake
  • New Free Truth Check
  • New Past Return Review
  • Fix a Tax Problem
  • Not sure / needs sorting

Business/risk flags

  • Needs business records or bookkeeping cleanup
  • Payroll tax issue
  • Sales tax issue
  • Trust-fund, levy, lien, garnishment, summons, court, or criminal warning
  • CPA/EA/accountant/bookkeeper/payroll/sales-tax/tax attorney review suggested

Amendment flags

  • Possible amended-return review
  • Federal/state/business amendment unknown
  • Refund deadline/statute warning
  • Paid amendment option only after review and user choice
  • No guarantee of refund, savings, or agency acceptance

Staff should confirm the free Truth Check Summary was delivered before any paid conversion path, and should not request unredacted taxpayer documents while live sensitive uploads are blocked.

Staff UX quality gate

Before follow-up, check the user experience.

Path clear?

Confirm the case is personal filing, business filing, prior-return review, Truth Check, tax problem, or not sure.

Free summary delivered?

Do not move a user into paid work until the free start is honored and the scope is clear.

Sensitive data protected?

Do not ask for full SSNs, bank data, transcripts, or unredacted notices while live upload gates are blocked.

Professional route?

Flag CPA, EA, PTIN, accountant, bookkeeper, payroll, sales-tax, or attorney review needs honestly.

Production should use staff accounts, MFA, role permissions, access logs, secure document storage, payment-webhook verification, quote approval, no-sensitive-details email rules, appointment confirmations, and professional review assignment controls.
