v0.1.64 release continuity

Improve the platform without risking customer, staff, professional, quote, payment, or document data.

This workbench turns data-preservation into a release gate. Every Justice Tax Solutions deployment should be a source-code update only — never a reset of accounts, login records, saved progress, uploaded document references, quotes, payments, staff notes, or professional work.

Homepage layout stability rule

The homepage structure is currently working well. Future polish should avoid major homepage redesigns unless Roger asks. Small refinements are allowed only when they remove duplicate links, clarify the start path, or reinforce save/resume and safety without changing the overall layout.

Review homepageData continuity

Public customers

Accounts, login details, cases, draft progress, uploaded document records, quote/payment status, and dashboard resume links must survive every deploy.

Staff and professionals

Staff notes, triage labels, accountant/CPA/EA/professional workpapers, assignments, checklists, signoffs, follow-up dates, and release gates must remain saved.

Owner/deployment

Back up before deploy, preserve persistent storage, avoid destructive migrations, smoke-test saved work after deploy, and record a release continuity check.

Release gates

Before deployment

  • Back up database and document/object storage.
  • Confirm DATA_DIR and UPLOAD_DIR are persistent or external.
  • Confirm quotes, payments, referral rewards, and webhook state are backed up.
  • Review schema changes for destructive migrations.
  • Prepare rollback and restore steps.

After deployment

  • Check /health version.
  • Sign in as a customer and open saved work.
  • Check staff queues, professional assignments, and work drafts.
  • Confirm uploaded document references and payment records still exist.
  • Record a release continuity check before inviting users back.

Save/resume promise

Long workflows should be resumable for public customers and professionals. Customers should see last-saved status and continue from the dashboard. Staff and professional reviewers should be able to pause work, save notes/checklists, and resume without losing progress.

Live sensitive taxpayer uploads remain blocked until production security gates pass. Drafts should not store full SSNs, EINs, bank/account numbers, full notices, transcripts, or uploaded document contents.
