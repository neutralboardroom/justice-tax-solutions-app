New York State Tax Notice Help

Get a clear next step for a New York State tax notice.

The platform helps sort the notice, collect the missing facts, flag deadlines, and route the matter to the lowest safe review level.

Who this helps

New Yorkers who received a Department of Taxation and Finance notice about income tax, residency, withholding, sales tax, assessment, penalty, or missing filing.

Likely routes

  • Notice summary
  • NYS return/amended return review
  • CPA review for residency/business facts
  • Tax attorney review when legal strategy is involved

Questions we may ask

  • What tax year is shown?
  • Is this about income, residency, sales tax, withholding, or a business filing?
  • Is there a deadline or assessment amount?
  • Have you moved in or out of New York?

When we escalate

AI should ask follow-up questions first, then route to the lowest safe review level. A customer can also request a professional simply for reassurance.

  • residency dispute
  • sales/payroll tax
  • warrant/collection language
  • large assessment
  • appeals deadline
  • business entity issue

Safe expectations

  • We are not New York State.
  • NYS deadlines and payment obligations are separate from platform fees.

Platform fees are separate from taxes, penalties, interest, government payments, and separate professional/legal fees where applicable.

How Justice Tax Solutions handles this

  1. Start with a plain-language intake.
  2. AI organizes facts and asks follow-up questions.
  3. The platform checks missing information, deadlines, and risk flags.
  4. Official forms remain gated until mapped, sample-filled, checked, and verified.
  5. PTIN, EA, CPA, or tax attorney review is added when required or requested.
