Referral program

Help someone start free with their tax concern.

Community partners can share a tracked link, QR code, and printable flyer. When an eligible referred client pays for a qualifying platform review, the referral can be credited.

1. Create an account

Each partner receives a unique referral code and tracked start link.

2. Print or share the flyer

The flyer includes your QR code so people can scan and start free.

3. Track activity

Your dashboard shows scans, accounts, cases, and eligible rewards.

Suggested rewards

Rewards apply to eligible platform review fees only. Government payments, taxes, penalties, interest, refunds, chargebacks, and third-party professional fees are excluded.
