Back Taxes and Unfiled Returns

Get organized when you have unfiled or prior-year tax returns.

Justice Tax Solutions helps organize each missing year, identify the records still needed, and determine whether preparation by a PTIN holder or review by an enrolled agent, CPA, or tax attorney may be appropriate.

Who this helps

People who missed one or more tax years, received missing return notices, or need help collecting W-2/1099/self-employment records.

Likely routes

  • Return organizer
  • PTIN review
  • CPA review for business/gig records
  • EA review if collection is active

Questions we may ask

  • Which years are missing?
  • Do you have W-2s or 1099s?
  • Were you self-employed?
  • Have you received IRS/NYS/NYC notices?
  • Do you need transcript help?

When we escalate

AI should ask follow-up questions first, then route to the lowest safe review level. A customer can also request a professional simply for reassurance.

  • multiple years
  • substitute return/SFR language
  • active collection
  • business income
  • missing records
  • large balance

Safe expectations

  • Organizing a return does not guarantee a refund, balance, or government decision.

Platform fees are separate from taxes, penalties, interest, government payments, and separate professional/legal fees where applicable.

How Justice Tax Solutions handles this

  1. Start with a plain-language intake.
  2. AI organizes facts and asks follow-up questions.
  3. The platform checks missing information, deadlines, and risk flags.
  4. Returns and supporting forms must be checked for accuracy and approved before filing.
  5. PTIN, EA, CPA, or tax attorney review is added when required or requested.
