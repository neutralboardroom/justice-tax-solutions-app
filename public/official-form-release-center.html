Official Form Release Center

Internal readiness center for controlled paid use of official IRS forms.

Important: A captured IRS PDF is not automatically ready for paying users. Each form, language, and revision must pass every operational, security, mapping, visual-QA, client-verification, professional-review, and owner-approval gate.

Release standard

  1. Official source and checksum verified
  2. AcroForm fields or overlay coordinates inventoried
  3. 100% semantic mapping for every used field
  4. Sample-filled output generated and hashed
  5. Page-by-page visual QA and overflow testing passed
  6. Signature and preparer fields controlled
  7. Client verification workflow tested
  8. Credentialed professional review workflow tested
  9. Production security controls passed
  10. Owner release approval recorded

What paid use means

When a specific form passes every gate, Justice Tax Solutions may offer guided preparation and controlled print-ready output for that approved form only. This does not activate IRS e-file, electronic agency transmission, guaranteed acceptance, or government approval.
