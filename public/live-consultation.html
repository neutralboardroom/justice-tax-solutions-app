Online professional completion

Get your tax problem or forms completed online with AI prep and the right professional level.

Justice Tax Solutions is being designed so the platform does the time-consuming intake, document organization, missing-question follow-up, field planning, and draft preparation before a professional joins. That keeps human work focused on review, judgment, correction, and final explanation. v0.1.28 keeps quote approval, safe payment requests, appointment confirmations, reminders, reschedule/cancel tracking, and marketing conversion pages so live sessions can be reached from public service funnels without bypassing safety gates.

Professional help can be required or requested

Some cases need a professional because of tax rules, collection risk, accounting complexity, or legal issues. Other customers may simply want a professional second look so they feel confident that the AI-prepared forms and answers are correct before printing, signing, filing, or responding.

The platform should honor customer-requested reassurance, explain the scope and cost, and avoid downgrading someone who asks for a higher professional level.

How the live online path should work

  1. Start free and choose tax notice, tax debt/back taxes, or return preparation/review.
  2. AI asks the full interview and follow-up questions based on your answers.
  3. Upload documents only when the production gates allow it, or use redacted/sample mode during demo/private pilot.
  4. The platform prepares source-linked fields, missing items, and draft forms where the official form mapping has been verified.
  5. A PTIN preparer, EA, CPA, or tax attorney reviews the level your facts require, or the level you request for reassurance and confidence.
  6. You review the summary, approve the next step, and receive print/signature or filing-support instructions when ready.

Live online completion is a planned/pilot service path. Final production use requires secure storage, malware scanning, reviewer verification, payment/webhook setup, official form QA, client approval, and operating procedures. No refund, debt reduction, agency outcome, or same-day representation is guaranteed.

Independent tax help. Not the IRS, NYS, NYC, or a law firm.
