How the live online path should work

  1. Start free and choose tax notice, tax debt/back taxes, or return preparation/review.
  2. AI asks the full interview and follow-up questions based on your answers.
  3. Upload documents only when the page clearly confirms secure document handling. Otherwise, start without files or use a properly redacted copy.
  4. The platform helps organize form information, identify missing items, and prepare review materials for supported workflows.
  5. A PTIN preparer, EA, CPA, or tax attorney reviews the level your facts require, or the level you request for reassurance and confidence.
  6. You review the summary, approve the next step, and receive print/signature or filing-support instructions when ready.

Availability of live online completion depends on the tax issue and the qualified professionals available. Secure document handling, taxpayer approval, and appropriate review are required. No refund, debt reduction, government outcome, or same-day representation is guaranteed.
