IRS Form 1040-X guided workspace

Amended individual return organizer for Form 1040-X.

Use this workspace to organize a possible amended U.S. individual income tax return after a prior Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR was filed. This is organizer, form preparation, and quality review support only. It is not final IRS output, e-file, agency submission, or a guarantee that amending will help.

Guided mode: We can help organize the amendment question, changed lines, supporting documents, deadline prompts, and professional-review routing. A completed Form 1040-X, signatures, preparer information, and IRS submission require verified information, taxpayer approval, and any appropriate professional review. Do not enter full Social Security numbers or IP PINs in ordinary fields.

When a 1040-X review may fit

Common reasons to review

  • Missed credits, deductions, dependents, education credits, or child-related credits.
  • Income-reporting, W-2, 1099, withholding, estimated-payment, or refundable-credit corrections.
  • Schedule C, self-employed, gig-worker, vehicle, home-office, depreciation, or business expense corrections.
  • IRS, NYS, NYC, or other notice connected to a filed return.

Important cautions

  • Amending is not always beneficial or appropriate.
  • Refund and amendment deadlines can matter.
  • State or city amended returns may also be needed.
  • Professional review may be needed before filing.

What this organizer can help you prepare

Original return details

Organize the return that was filed, the tax year, filing status, and the items that may need correction.

Changes and supporting records

List each proposed change and the records that support the corrected amount.

Explanation and next steps

Prepare a plain-language explanation and identify any related federal, state, or city return that may also need review.

This organizer does not file an amended return. A completed Form 1040-X requires verified information, taxpayer approval, and any appropriate professional review.

What must be verified before paid amendment preparation

  • Original filed return and corrected return are available.
  • Each changed amount has support and a clear Part II explanation.
  • Changed schedules/forms are identified and reviewed.
  • Federal refund deadline and state/city amendment impact are reviewed.
