Fix a tax return mistake with a safer review path.

Who this helps

People who need to correct income, filing status, deductions, credits, dependents, state issues, or Schedule C/business items.

Likely routes

  • 1040-X organizer
  • PTIN review
  • CPA review
  • EA review for notice/collection tie-ins
  • Attorney review for legal-sensitive corrections

Questions we may ask

  • Which year needs correction?
  • What changed?
  • Did the IRS or state already send a notice?
  • Do you have the original return?
  • Did you already receive a refund?

When we escalate

AI should ask follow-up questions first, then route to the lowest safe review level. A customer can also request a professional simply for reassurance.

  • large refund claim
  • EITC/credit dispute
  • business expense changes
  • audit notice
  • fraud/misrepresentation concern

Safe expectations

  • No refund, acceptance, or processing timeline is guaranteed.

Platform fees are separate from taxes, penalties, interest, government payments, and separate professional/legal fees where applicable.

How Justice Tax Solutions handles this

  1. Start with a plain-language intake.
  2. AI organizes facts and asks follow-up questions.
  3. The platform checks missing information, deadlines, and risk flags.
  4. Official forms remain gated until mapped, sample-filled, checked, and verified.
  5. PTIN, EA, CPA, or tax attorney review is added when required or requested.

Independent tax help. Not the IRS, NYS, NYC, or a law firm.
