Tax notice help

Got a tax letter? Do not panic, but do not ignore it.

Use this page to organize the first facts before you call, upload, pay, or respond. Justice Tax Solutions can help you understand the starting point and decide whether AI-only organization, PTIN review, EA/CPA review, or tax attorney review may be appropriate.

Pilot safety rule: do not upload real unredacted tax notices, full SSNs, bank information, transcripts, W-2s, 1099s, or full tax returns until secure live upload approval is complete. You can start with no file or a redacted/sample copy.

Find these items first

  • Agency name: IRS, New York State, or NYC
  • Notice number or letter code
  • Tax year or tax period
  • Date on the notice and response deadline
  • Amount due or proposed change, if any
  • What the agency is asking you to do

First 15 minutes

  1. Keep the notice and envelope together.
  2. Write down the agency, notice number, tax year, amount, and deadline.
  3. Check whether the letter asks for payment, documents, identity verification, an amended return, or an explanation.
  4. Do not assume the agency is right, but do not miss the deadline.
  5. Use official agency contact information or ask for professional review before responding to risky notices.

Escalate faster if you see this

  • Levy, lien, wage garnishment, bank hold, seizure, or summons
  • Audit, appeals, fraud, criminal, or court language
  • Same-day, next-day, or very short deadline
  • Large balance you cannot pay
  • Identity-theft or account-access issue
  • Business payroll, sales tax, trust-fund, or employee withholding issue

Choose the closest path
