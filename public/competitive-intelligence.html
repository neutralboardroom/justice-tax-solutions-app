What we learned from tax competitors

Justice Tax Solutions is being built as a tax-problem-first platform. We learn from consumer tax software, tax-resolution firms, professional prep systems, document automation platforms, and newer AI tax tools, but we adapt the lessons for anxious taxpayers who need notices, tax debt, back taxes, New York/NYC issues, and human review.

Our rule

Adapt the helpful mechanics: guided interview, document upload, field source map, workpaper binder, professional review, live online completion, and clear status tracking. Avoid refund guarantees, OIC promises, ghost-preparer risk, difficult downgrades, and black-box AI tax answers.
