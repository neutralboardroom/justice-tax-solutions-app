Full-platform UX audit

Polish check for public users, clients, staff, and owner review.

This page summarizes the v0.1.59 platform-wide language and UX pass. It is an internal review page, not a customer promise that sensitive uploads, official form output, e-file, payments, or professional operations are fully live.

What this audit checks

  • Homepage and start path clarity.
  • Customer-facing language and no-guarantee wording.
  • Personal filing, business filing, prior-return review, Free Truth Check, and tax-problem visibility.
  • Dashboard next-step guidance.
  • Staff triage language and queue fields.
  • Spanish touchpoints and document-safety reminders.

What remains blocked

  • Live sensitive taxpayer uploads.
  • Final official IRS/NYS/NYC form output.
  • E-file, agency submission, direct debit, Refund Transfer, and refund advances.
  • Guaranteed refunds, settlements, savings, amendment benefits, payment plans, or agency outcomes.
  • Automatic CPA, EA, attorney, or professional review claims without assignment and approval.

Customer language standard

Clear, calm, careful, and useful.

Use “may,” “possible,” “worth reviewing,” “could help,” and “professional review may be needed.” Do not use fear-based tax-resolution claims, government-like language, or promises that a return, amendment, payment plan, penalty request, or refund will be accepted.

Start paths

File a personal return, file a business return, review past returns, start a Free Truth Check, fix a tax problem, or choose “not sure.”

Free vs paid

Truth Check and first screening are free starting points. Paid filing, preparation, amendment work, or professional review happens only if the user chooses it after scope and price are explained.

Safety mode

No-file and redacted/sample-only starts remain the safe default. Users should not upload originals, SSNs, bank details, full returns, transcripts, or unredacted notices.
