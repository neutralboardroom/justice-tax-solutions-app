Who this helps

Taxpayers who need to disclose income, expenses, assets, debts, bank accounts, vehicles, and household financial facts for IRS collection review.

Likely routes

  • 433-F organizer
  • EA review
  • CPA review for business/self-employed records
  • Attorney review for legal-sensitive collection facts

Questions we may ask

  • What is your monthly income?
  • What are your necessary living expenses?
  • Do you own vehicles or real estate?
  • Do you have bank/investment accounts?
  • Are you self-employed?

When we escalate

AI should ask follow-up questions first, then route to the lowest safe review level. A customer can also request a professional simply for reassurance.

  • business ownership
  • missing bank records
  • asset transfers
  • payroll tax
  • levy/lien
  • legal collection dispute

Safe expectations

  • Financial disclosure forms should be checked carefully before signing or sending to the IRS.

How Justice Tax Solutions handles this

  1. Start with a plain-language intake.
