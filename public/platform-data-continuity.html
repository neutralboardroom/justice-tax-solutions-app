v0.1.64 data continuity

Save progress and preserve user data through every deployment.

Justice Tax Solutions must treat user accounts, work progress, staff notes, professional review work, uploaded document references, payment records, and saved drafts as persistent business records — not source-code files that can be overwritten by a new ZIP.

Non-negotiable deployment rule

Deployments are source-code updates only. They must never wipe customer accounts, login details, partially completed work, uploaded documents, staff/accountant/CPA work, quotes, payments, review notes, or audit records.

For customers

Long tax workflows should be saveable at any point. Customers should be able to stop, return later, and see what was already entered or completed.

For staff and professionals

Staff, accountants, CPAs, EAs, bookkeepers, payroll/sales-tax specialists, and tax attorneys need saved notes, assignments, workpapers, signoffs, and follow-up dates across deployments.

For owner/deployment

Use persistent database/object storage or external data/upload paths. Back up before deploy and smoke-test saved accounts, drafts, documents, payments, and queues after deploy.
