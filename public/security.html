Security

Tax documents are sensitive. We treat that as a core product requirement.

Current foundation

The platform includes account/session basics, encrypted local document persistence, access logging, referral tools, Stripe-ready payments, professional compliance gates, and staff review workflows.

Before full public launch

Live sensitive tax-document handling should use managed PostgreSQL, private object storage, signed upload URLs, malware scanning, staff/professional roles, MFA, audit logs, retention/deletion rules, and strict review permissions.

Do not email full tax documents or full Social Security numbers unless a secure process is in place.

Security is part of the tax workflow, not an afterthought.
