Security

Tax documents are sensitive. We treat that as a core product requirement.

Current foundation

The platform uses account controls, access logging, role-based review, and payment records to support the service. Sensitive-document availability depends on the specific workflow and its security requirements.

How to protect your information

Do not send sensitive tax documents through ordinary email or free-text fields. Use a designated secure upload only when it is explicitly offered, and remove unnecessary Social Security numbers, bank information, and account details whenever possible.

Do not email full tax documents or full Social Security numbers unless a secure process is in place.

Security is part of the tax workflow, not an afterthought.
