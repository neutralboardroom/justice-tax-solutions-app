Privacy

Justice Tax Solutions should collect only information needed to understand the tax concern, organize documents, contact the user, provide requested review services, and track authorized referral attribution.

Before production launch, secure persistent file storage, access controls, retention policies, encryption practices, professional-review permissions, and deletion/export workflows must be finalized.

Users should not enter full Social Security numbers, full bank account numbers, or other highly sensitive details into early-stage free-text fields unless secure production storage and review procedures are active.

Referral codes may be used to attribute where a user started. Referral partners should receive attribution counts and reward status, not private tax details, unless the user separately authorizes that disclosure.
