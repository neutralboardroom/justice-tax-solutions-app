Privacy

Justice Tax Solutions should collect only information needed to understand the tax concern, organize documents, contact the user, provide requested review services, and track authorized referral attribution.

Sensitive documents are accepted only through workflows that clearly indicate secure document handling is available. Access is limited according to role and service need, and retention or deletion requests are handled under the applicable policy and legal requirements.

Do not enter full Social Security numbers, full bank account numbers, or other highly sensitive details into ordinary free-text fields. Use only a designated secure workflow when it is explicitly offered.

Referral codes may be used to attribute where a user started. Referral partners should receive attribution counts and reward status, not private tax details, unless the user separately authorizes that disclosure.
