Your past returns may be worth a second look.

Start a review of a recent personal or business return. Our AI-assisted workflow can organize the information and flag possible missed credits, deductions, reporting issues, filing-status concerns, business expenses, or other items for human tax review.

What the review can examine

Possible missed deductions or credits, incorrect or omitted income, dependents, filing status, education or child-related credits, self-employment expenses, business-return issues, and notices connected to a previously filed return.

What happens next

The initial amendment-opportunity screening is a free starting point. If an amended return may be appropriate, we explain the documents, review level, and any paid preparation options before you decide whether to continue.

Amending a return is not always beneficial or appropriate. Deadlines and tax consequences can matter. A qualified tax professional should review whether an amendment is appropriate before filing. No refund, savings, acceptance, or result is guaranteed.
