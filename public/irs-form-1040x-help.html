Amended return help for IRS Form 1040-X.

The platform organizes the change reason, supporting documents, changed numbers, and professional review level before any client-ready amended return package is released.

Who this helps

Taxpayers who need to change a filed return and want a clear explanation of what changed, why, and what support documents are needed.

Likely routes

  • 1040-X organizer
  • PTIN review
  • CPA review
  • EA review if notice/collection connected

Questions we may ask

  • What year are you amending?
  • What changed?
  • Do you have the original return?
  • Was there a notice?
  • Do you expect a refund or additional balance?

When we escalate

AI should ask follow-up questions first, then route to the lowest safe review level. A customer can also request a professional simply for reassurance.

  • large refund
  • tax credit changes
  • business changes
  • audit notice
  • prior amendments

Safe expectations

  • No refund or acceptance guarantee. Processing times are controlled by the agency.

Platform fees are separate from taxes, penalties, interest, government payments, and separate professional/legal fees where applicable.

How Justice Tax Solutions handles this

  1. Start with a plain-language intake.
