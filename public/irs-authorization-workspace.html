Form 2848

Power of Attorney and Declaration of Representative. Use when an eligible representative may need to act before the IRS for listed matters and tax periods.

  • Requires representative eligibility review.
  • Each taxpayer needs a separate authorization.
  • Signature and declaration fields remain final-only.

Form 8821

Tax Information Authorization. Use when someone may inspect or receive confidential tax information but is not authorized to represent the taxpayer before the IRS.

  • Requires designee and scope review.
  • Does not authorize representation.
  • Signature fields remain final-only.
