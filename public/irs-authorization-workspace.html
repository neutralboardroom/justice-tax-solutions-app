Organize IRS authorization forms before staff or professional review.

This workspace covers IRS Form 2848 and Form 8821 in controlled organizer mode. Users can answer the non-sensitive organizer questions on the website, but final IRS authorization output, signatures, Tax Pro Account submission, fax/mail submission, and representation claims remain blocked until all review, identity, signature, security, and official-form QA gates pass.

Do not enter full SSNs, ITINs, EINs, PTINs, signatures, or confidential account data here yet. Use last-4 identifiers or placeholders only until production sensitive-data and signature controls are approved.

Form 2848

Power of Attorney and Declaration of Representative. Use when an eligible representative may need to act before the IRS for listed matters and tax periods.

  • Requires representative eligibility review.
  • Each taxpayer needs a separate authorization.
  • Signature and declaration fields remain final-only.

Form 8821

Tax Information Authorization. Use when someone may inspect or receive confidential tax information but is not authorized to represent the taxpayer before the IRS.

  • Requires designee and scope review.
  • Does not authorize representation.
  • Signature fields remain final-only.
